Short title
This Act may be cited as the Personal Income Tax (Amendment) Act 2016.

In this Act –

4\. Amendment of section17
Section 17 of the principal Act is amended –
(a)

at subsection (1) by repealing the words, “not later than fifteen 15 days after the
last day of each month” and substituting the words, “not later than the 15th
August, 2016”;

(b)

at subsection (2) by repealing the words, “not later than the 15th February in each
year” and by substituting the words, “not later than the 15th August, 2016”;

(c)

at subsection (3)(b) by repealing the words, “not later than the 15th February in
each year or twenty-one days after he ceases to be the employer of a particular
employee” and substituting the words, “not later than the 15th August, 2016”.

5\. Amendment of section 23
Section 23 of the principal Act is amended at subsection (1) by repealing the words “in an income
year shall, not later than 31st March in each year,” and substituting the words, “for income year
2016 shall, not later than 30th September, 2016”
