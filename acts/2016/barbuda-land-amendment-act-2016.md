4\. Amendment of section 8- Conversion of rights into leases
Section 8 of the principal Act is amended by the repeal of subsections (1) and (2) and the
substitution of the following—
“(1) A person to whom an exclusive right of occupation has been granted by the Council may,
after five years of occupancy, apply to the Council for the grant of lease for the purposes for
which the exclusive right was granted.
(2) The Council may grant a lease under this section for a maximum term of ninety-nine years
and any term so granted may be subject to renewal.”
5\. Amendment of section 11-Administration and development of land
Section 11 of the Principal Act is amended by the repeal of paragraph (c) of subsection (1) and the
substitution of the following—
“(c) the granting of lease of land in Barbuda in accordance with provisions of this Act”.
