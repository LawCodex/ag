8\. Use and sharing of API with regional and international security agencies
(1) IMPACS shall use the APIS to conduct screening against Watch Lists of members of crew
and passengers on aircraft and vessels that enter into, depart from and travel within the regional
space in order to provide information to assist participating countries.
(2) IMPACS may share the information contained within APIS with INTERPOL and any other
national, regional or international intelligence, law enforcement or security agencies or centres
approved by CONSLE in order to further national, regional or international security.
(3) API shall only be used for the purposes mentioned in this Act.

9\. Retention of API
(1) The API collected under this Act for entry screening purposes shall be retained for a period
not exceeding three years from the date of travel of the crew or the passenger.
(2) Nothing contained in subsection (1) shall apply to the data copied from the APIS into any
other security database system to which a different data retention schedule applies.
10\. Regulations
(1) The Minister may make regulations to give effect to the principles and provisions of this
Act.

(2) The Minister may make regulations in regard to procedures for the gathering of information
and for the collaboration and sharing of information with the agencies mentioned in this Act.
