No. 18 of 2016
AN ACT to amend the Evidence (Special Provisions) Act 2009 No.5 of 2009 in order to confer on
officers of The National Drug and Money Laundering Policy certain powers already conferred
police officers in the said Act
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows1. Short Title
This Act may be cited as Evidence (Special Provisions) (Amendment) (No. 2) Act 2016
2\. Amendment of section 56 of Act 2009 No.5
Section 56 of the Evidence (Special Provisions) Act 2009 No. 5 of 2009 is amended(a)

in subsection (1) thereof by deleting the words “issue a warrant in writing
authorising an officer of the Royal Police Force of Antigua and Barbuda”
appearing in lines one and two thereof, and substituting the following —

“upon application by way of fixed date claim supported by affidavit issue a warrant in writing
to an officer of the Royal Police Force of Antigua and Barbuda Police or an officer of the
ONDCP”;

(b)

Evidence (Special Provisions) (Amendment) (No. 2) Act, 2016.

in subsection (5) by inserting the words “an officer of the ONDCP” after the words

“special police constable” appearing in line two thereof.
