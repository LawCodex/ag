ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
FIREARMS (AMENDMENT) ACT 2016
No. 19 of 2016
AN ACT to amend the Firearm Act, Cap 171 in order to grant the Director and officers of the
ONDCP an exemption with respect to firearm user’s licence.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows—

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Firearms (Amendment) Act, 2016.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act “principal Act” means the Firearms Act, Cap. 171.

### 3.

Amendment of section 6(2) of the principal Act
Section 6(2) of the principal Act is amended—
(a)

by deleting the word “or” appearing in line 7 and substituting therefor a comma
(“,”); and

(b)

Firearms (Amendment) Act, 2016.

by deleting the full stop appearing at the end of the said subsection and
substituting therefor the following “ or the Director and officers of the ONDCP in
the execution of their functions under the Office of National Drug and Money
Laundering Control Policy Act.”

### 4.

Amendment of section 7 (1) (a) of the principal Act.
Paragraph (a) of section 7(1) of the principal Act is amended—
(a)

deleting the word “or” appearing in line 2 thereof and substituting therefor a
comma (“,”); and

(b)

by inserting after the words “as such” appearing in line 3 thereof the following “ or
the Director and officers of the ONDCP acting in their capacities as such ”.
