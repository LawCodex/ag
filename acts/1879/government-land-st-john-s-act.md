GOVERNMENT LAND (ST. JOHN'S)
(14th November, 1879.)

Whereas doubts have arisen as to the boundaries of the
plot known as the Government land to the east of the city
of St. John's, and it is expedient that the same should be
defined and put under certain regulation.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Government Land Short title.
(St. John's) Act.

### 3.

The said Government land shall be under the control of the Cabinet and shall be used for such purposes as
shall from time to time be sanctioned by rules to be passed
by the Cabinet, which rules, on being approved by Parliament, shall be of the same effect as if they had been incorporated in this Act.
