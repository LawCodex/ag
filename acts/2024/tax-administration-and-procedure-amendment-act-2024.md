No. 3 of 2024
AN ACT to amend the Tax Administration and Procedure Act 2018, No. 12 of 2018 and for other
connected purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows―

---

### 1.

Short title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Tax Administration and Procedure (Amendment) Act 2024.
(2) This Act shall come into operation on the 1st day of April 2024.

### 2.

Interpretation

In this Act –
“principal Act” means the Tax Administration and Procedure Act 2018, No. 12 of 2018.

### 3.

Amendment of Schedule 1

Schedule 1 of the principal Act is amended to insert the following Act into the schedule –
“Money Services Business (Transfer) Levy Act”
