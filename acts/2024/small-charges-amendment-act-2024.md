ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
SMALL CHARGES (AMENDMENT) ACT 2024
No. 20 of 2024
AN ACT to amend the Small Charges Act, Cap. 405 to maintain public safety and the safety
of children by discouraging the practice of children loitering and requiring them to be
accompanied by a parent between the hours of 10pm to 6am and for other connected
purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows―

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Small Charges (Amendment) Act 2024.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“principal Act” means the Small Charges Act Cap 405;
“loitering” for the purposes of Sections 11A and 11B means to linger or hang around in a
public place or business where one has no particular or legal purpose for being present.

### 3.

Insertion of section 11A - Prohibition against loitering
The principal Act is amended by inserting immediately after section 11 the following—

“11A. Prohibition against loitering
(1). A person under the age of 18 years shall not be permitted to hang around, loiter or
congregate on the streets or in any public place in Antigua and Barbuda between the hours of 6am
to 10 pm., except when he or she is participating in a lawful activity conducted by a registered
association such as a football club, netball club, basketball club or similar association for the
practice of a recognised sport, whether such association is affiliated with the community or an
educational institution.
(2) A person under the age of 18 years who contravenes subsection (1), commits an offence.
(3) A police officer who finds a child under the age of 18 years loitering in the street or a public
place may –
(a)

take the child to the home of the parents and warn either of the parents of the
consequences of the offence being repeated;

(b)

take the child to the police station and have a parent of the child collect the child at
the police station; or

(c)

charge the child with the offence of loitering under subsection (2).

### 4.

Insertion of section 11B - Offence of loitering of child at night
The principal Act is amended by inserting immediately after section 11A the following—
“11B. Offence of loitering of child at night
(1) A person under the age of 18 years shall not be permitted to be loitering on the streets or
in any public place between the hours of 10 o’clock in the night and 6 o’clock in the morning
unless accompanied by a parent or a person acting in loco parentis of the child.
(2) A parent whose child is found loitering in the street or in a public place shall be warned
of the consequences of the offence being repeated.”
(3) If having been warned in accordance with subsection (2), a child is found loitering in the
street or in a public place contrary to subsection (1), then the parent of the child commits an
offence and is liable on summary conviction –
(a)

if it is the first time that the parent is being charged with the offence, to a fine of
two thousand ($2,000.) dollars; or

(b)

if it is the second time that the parent is being charged for the offence, to a fine of
three thousand ($3,000.) dollars; or

(c)

where the parent is being for the third time or any subsequent occurrence, to a fine
of five thousand ($5,000.) dollars and to imprisonment for a term of six months.

(4) In this section, parent includes a person who has custody of the child.”

### 5.

Insertion of section 11C – Offence to allow child to loiter in a nightclub etc.
The principal Act is amended by inserting immediately after Section 11B the following –
“11C. Offence of allowing a child to loiter in a nightclub etc.
(1) An owner, operator or person who controls a nightclub, bar, fete or similar place shall not
permit a person who is under the age of 18 years to loiter in the nightclub, bar, fete or similar
place.
(2) A person who contravenes subsection (1) commits an offence and is liable on summary
conviction to a fine of five thousand ($5,000.) dollars.”
