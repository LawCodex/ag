MAGISTRATE’S CODE OF PROCEDURE (AMENDMENT) (NO. 2) ACT 2024
No. 24 of 2024
AN ACT to amend the Magistrate’s Code of Procedure Act, Cap. 255 and for other connected
purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows―

---

### 1.

Short title

This Act may be cited as the Magistrate’s Code of Procedure (Amendment) (No. 2) Act 2024.

### 2.

Interpretation

In this Act –
"principal Act” means the Magistrate’s Code of Procedure Act, Cap. 255

### 3.

Amendment of section 250 – Power to make rules

Section 250 of the principal Act is amended as follows:

No. 24 of 2024.

(a) by the repeal of subsection (1);
(b) by repealing subsection (2) and replacing it as follows –
“(2) The Minister may make rules regulating the following–
(a)

operating procedures in the Magistrate’s Court;

(b)

the forms of security acceptable under this Act;

(c)

holding of fines, fees and other sums paid into Court;

(d)

provision of statements of accounts relating to fines, fees and other sums
received or paid into Court; and

(e)

the opening of accounts for monies paid into the Court in relation to any matter
and the closing of any account;

(c) by repealing subsections (3) and (4) thereof and replacing these as follows –
“(3) The Chief Justice and any two other Judges of the Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court
selected by the Chief Justice may make rules regulating the practice and procedure to be
observed in the Magistrate’s Court.
(4) The rules made pursuant to subsection (3) may make provision for –
(a)

the procedure to be followed in making any claim or application in magisterial
proceedings;

(b)

service of a claim or application, answering and replying to a claim or
application;

(c)

the Forms to be used under this Act;

(d)

proceeding filed on the electronic litigation portal;

(e)

fees payable for filing of documents;

(f)

issuing practice directions and guidelines; and

(g)

any proceedings in the Magistrate’s Court for which rules are required to be
made.”

(d) by repealing subsection (6) in its entirety.
