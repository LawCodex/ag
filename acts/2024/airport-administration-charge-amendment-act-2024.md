### 1.

Short title and commencement
(1)The Act may be cited as the Airport Administration Charge (Amendment) Act, 2024.
(2)This Act shall come into effect on the 1st day of October, 2024.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act ―
“principal Act” means the Airport Administration Charge Act 201, No. 3 of 2012
