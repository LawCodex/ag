ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
REVENUE (MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS) ACT 2024
No. 5 of 2024
AN ACT to amend various tax laws to remove the discretionary power of the Minister to waive
taxes and to provide the infrastructural framework for accounting for waivers of customs duties
and for other connected purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows―

---

### 1.

Short title
(1) This Act may be cited as the Revenue (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2024.
(2) This Act comes into force from the 1st April, 2024

### 2.

Amendment of the Environmental Protection Levy Act 2002, No. 22 of 2002
The Environmental Protection Levy Ac 2002 is amended by the repeal of section 6 thereof.

### 4.

Amendment of the Tourism Guest Levy Act 2021, No. 13 of 2021
The Tourism Guest Levy Act 2021 is amended by the repeal of section 10 thereof.

### 5.

Amendment of the Customs (Control and Management) Act 2013, No. 3 of 2013
The Customs (Control and Management) Act 2013 is amended in section 71, by repealing
subsections (4), (5) and (6) thereof and replacing these as follows –
“(4) The Minister may by Order exempt imported or exported goods from payment of any
part or the whole of the Customs duties, but such exemption shall not be granted for goods
imported for retail.
(5) The Minister shall cause a report to be prepared twice per year, being the 1st January and the
1st July each year, containing information on the exemption of duties granted in the preceding six
months, the purpose for the exemption and the amount thereof, and shall as soon as practicable,
but not less than 45 days after the end of a six-month period, cause the report to be laid before
Parliament and a copy thereof sent to the Public Accounts Committee.
(6) All import or export customs duties and all exemptions from customs duties granted prior to
the coming into force of this Act, shall continue in force until revoked or reduced and are deemed
to have been validly and properly granted.”
