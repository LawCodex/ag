ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
MIDWIFERY (AMENDMENT) ACT 2024
No. 16 of 2024
AN ACT to amend the Midwifery Act, Cap. 281 and for other connected purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

---

### 1.

Short title

This Act may be cited as the Midwifery (Amendment) Act, 2024.

### 2.

Interpretation

In this Act—
“principal Act” means the Midwifery Act, Cap. 281.

Amendment of section 3 – Constitution of Midwives’ Board

Section 3 of the principal Act is repealed and replaced as follows –
“3. Constitution of Midwives’ Board

Midwifery (Amendment) Act, 2024

(1) For the purposes of this Act, there shall be established a Board to be known as the
Midwives’ Board which shall consist of –
(a)

the Chief Medical Officer, who shall be Chairperson of the Board;

(b)

the Principal Nursing Officer, who shall be the Deputy Chairperson;

(c)

the Director of Nursing at the Sir Lester Bird Medical Centre;

(d)

the Head of the Midwifery Training Programme at the Antigua State College;

(e)

One person appointed by the Nurses Association;

(f)

Two persons appointed by the Cabinet.

(2) The members appointed in accordance with paragraphs (e) and (f) of subsection (1) shall
hold office for such term as the Cabinet may determine.
(3) Cabinet may revoke the appointment of a member who is unable to perform the duties
required of the Board and may fill any vacancy arising from the revocation of the
appointment of the member or arising from death, illness or other inability of a member to
function on the Board.
(4) A member appointed in accordance with paragraphs (e) and (f) of subsection (1) may resign
the appointment in writing addressed to the Cabinet Secretary.
(5) The appointment, removal or resignation of a member shall be published in the Gazette.”

### 4.

Amendment of section 4 – Meetings of the Board

Section 4 of the principal Act is amended –
(a)

by deleting the word “chairman” wheresoever it occurs throughout the section and
replacing this with the word “chairperson”.

(b)

by repealing subsection (3) and replacing it with the following –
“(3) Five members of the Board shall constitute a quorum.”

### 5.

Amendment of section 5 – Secretary to the Board

Section 5 of the principal Act is repealed and replaced as follows –
“5. Secretary to the Board
The Cabinet may appoint some fit and proper person to be Secretary to the Board
who shall perform such duties as the Board may direct.”

Amendment of section 18 – Midwives’ districts

Section 18 of the principal Act is amended in subsection (3) by repealing the words “Governor
General” and replacing these with the word “Minister”.

### 7.

Amendment of section 27 – Power to make rules

Section 27 of the principal Act is amended in subsection (2) by repealing the word “chairman” and
replacing it with the word “chairperson”.
