COMPANIES (AMENDMENT) (NO. 2) ACT 2024
No. 25 of 2024

AN ACT to amend the Companies Act 1995, No. 18 of 1995 and for other connected purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

---

### 1.

Short title

This Act may be cited as the Companies (Amendment) (No. 2) Act, 2024.

### 2.

Interpretation

In this Act—
“principal Act” means the Companies Act 1995, No. 18 of 1995.

### 3.

Amendment of section 66 – Disqualified Directors

Section 66 of the principal Act is amended as follows –
(a)

by repealing subsection (2) thereof and replacing it as follows –

“(2) An individual who has served for ten consecutive years –

No. 25 of 2024.

(a)

as a director of a public company;

(b)

as a director of any subsidiary of a public company; or

(c)

in aggregate, as a director of a public company and any of its subsidiaries,

shall not be qualified for re-election as a director of that company or of any of its subsidiaries
unless a period of two years has elapsed after he last served.”
(b)

in subsection (3), by inserting after the words, “public company”, the words, “or of
any subsidiary of a public company”.
