No. 11 of 2012

AN ACT to amend the Antigua and Barbuda Sales Tax Act, 2006 and for incidental and connected
purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Antigua and Barbuda Sales Tax (Amendment) Act, 2012.

### 2.

Interpretation

—————

In this Act “the Principal Act” means the Antigua and Barbuda Sales Tax Act 2006, No. 5 of 2006.
