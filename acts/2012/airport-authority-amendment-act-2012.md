ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
THE AIRPORT AUTHORITY (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2012
No. 2 of 2012

AN ACT to amend the Airport Authority Act 2006, No. 17 of 2006 and for incidental and
connected purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:
1.

Short title

This Act may be cited as the Airport Authority (Amendment) Act, 2012.
2.

Interpretation

In this Act the principal Act means the Airport Authority Act 2006, No. 17 of 2006.
3.

Amendment of section 4 – Functions of Authority

The principal Act is amended in subsection 4—
(a) in paragraph (2)(h) by repealing the word “.” after the words “required by airlines”
and substituting the words “; and”; and
(b) inserting after paragraph (h) the following paragraph—

“(i) manage the proceeds generated pursuant to the Airport Administration Charge
Act, 2012 in accordance with that Act.”.
4.

Amendment of section 10 – Funds and resources

The principal Act is amended in subsection 10—
(a) in paragraph (1)(d) by repealing the word “.” after the words “of the Authority” and
substituting the words “; and”; and
(b) inserting after paragraph (d) the following paragraph—
“(e) the proceeds generated pursuant to the Airport Administration Charge Act,
2012.”.
