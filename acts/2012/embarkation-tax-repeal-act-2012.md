ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
THE EMBARKATION TAX (REPEAL) ACT, 2012
No. 5 of 2012
AN ACT to repeal the Embarkation Tax Act 2002, No. 1 of 2002 and for incidental and connected
purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:
1.

Short title

This Act may be cited as the Embarkation Tax (Repeal) Act, 2012.
2.

Repeal

The Embarkation Tax Act 2002 is repealed.
