No. 10 of 2012
AN ACT to prescribe general principles in accordance with section 64 (3) of Schedule I to the Antigua
and Barbuda Constitution Order 1981 to guide the Constituencies Boundaries Commission when it
reviews the number, and the boundaries, of constituencies and makes its report thereon and for
incidental and connected purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Constituencies Boundaries Commission Guidance Act, 2012.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act the “Commission” means the Constituencies Boundaries Commission established by
section 63(1) of Schedule I to The Antigua and Barbuda Constitution Order 1981, Cap. 23.
