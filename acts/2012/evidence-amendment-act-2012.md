AN ACT to amend the Evidence Act, Cap. 155 and for incidental and connected purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Evidence (Amendment) Act, 2012.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“principal Act” means the Evidence Act, Cap. 155;
“Director of the ONDCP” means the person holding the position of Director under section 3 of the
ONDCP Act 2003;

3\. Amendment of section 12C
The principal Act is amended in section 12C—
(1) By inserting, in subsection (1) (b) the words “or an ONDCP officer” after the words “police
officer”.
(2) By inserting, in subsection (3) the words “or an ONDCP Manager” after the words “Gazetted
Officer” wherever they appear.
(3) By inserting, in subsection (5) the words “or an ONDCP Manager” after the words “Gazetted
Officer”.
(4) By inserting, in subsection (6) the words “or the Director of the ONDCP” after the words
“Commissioner of Police”.
(5) By inserting, in subsection (7) the words “or the Director of the ONDCP” after the word
“Commissioner”.
4\. Amendment of section 12E
The principal Act is amended in section 12E—
(1) By inserting in the heading to the section, the words “or Director of the ONDCP” after the word
“Commissioner”.
(2) By inserting the words “or the Director of the ONDCP” after the words “Commissioner of
Police”.
5\. Amendment of Second Schedule
The principal Act is amended in the Second Schedule by inserting the words “/ONDCP Manager”
after the words “Gazetted Officer” wherever they appear.
