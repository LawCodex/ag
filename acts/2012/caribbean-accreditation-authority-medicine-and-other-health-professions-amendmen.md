No. 14 of 2012

AN ACT to amend the Caribbean Accreditation Authority (Medicine and Other Health
Professions) Act, 2009, No. 4 of 2009.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

---

### 1.

Short title.

This Act may be cited as The Caribbean Accreditation Authority (Medicine and Other Health
Professions) (Amendment) Act, 2012.

### 2.

Section 4 repealed and replaced – Implementation of the Agreement.

Section 4 of the Caribbean Accreditation Authority (Medicine and Other Health Professions) Act,
2010 is repealed and replaced by the following –
(1) The Authority is hereby authorised to accredit programmes of study in medicine and other
health professions offered by institutions in Antigua and Barbuda.

(2) The Minister may, after consultation with the Authority, make such regulations, as he
considers necessary or expedient to give effect to the Agreement.
