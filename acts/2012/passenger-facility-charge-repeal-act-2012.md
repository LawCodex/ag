ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
THE PASSENGER FACILITY CHARGE (REPEAL) ACT, 2012
No. 6 of 2012

AN ACT to repeal the Passenger Facility Charge Act 2002, No. 21 of 2002 and for incidental and
connected purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:
1.

Short title

This Act may be cited as the Passenger Facility Charge (Repeal) Act, 2012.
2.

Interpretation

In this Act the principal Act means the Passenger Facility Charge Act 2002, No. 21 of 2002.
3.

Transition and repeal

(1) Any monies remaining in the Passenger Facility Charge Fund prior to the commencement
date of the Airport Administration Charge Act, 2012 shall be deposited in the account designated
by the Authority for the Airport Administration Charge pursuant to the Airport Authority Act,
2006 No. 17 of 2006.

(2) Section 43(10) of the Finance Administration Act, 2006 No. 23 of 2006 shall not apply to
the monies referred to under subsection (1).
(3) The principal Act is repealed.
