(1) For the purposes of this section—
(a)

“valid licence” includes a valid driver’s licence, valid learner’s permit or valid public
service vehicle driver’s permit; and

(b)

“vehicle” includes a motor cycle, a private motor car, a light or heavy goods vehicle, a
land or motor tractor, a public service vehicle;

as referred to in Part II of the Transport Board Regulations, 1999.
(2) A person shall not drive a vehicle on any road unless he is the holder of a valid licence.
(3) A person who contravenes subsection (2) commits and offence and is liable on summary
conviction to imprisonment for two years and a fine of five thousand dollars.”.
