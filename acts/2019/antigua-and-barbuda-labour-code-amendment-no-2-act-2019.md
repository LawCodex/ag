No. 26 of 2019
AN ACT to amend the Antigua and Barbuda Labour Code, Cap. 27 and for other incidental and
connected purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows –

---

### 1.

Short title

This Act may be cited as the Antigua and Barbuda Labour Code (Amendment) (No. 2) Act, 2019.

### 2.

Interpretation

In this Act, “the Principal Act” means the Antigua and Barbuda Labour Code, Cap. 27.

### 3.

Amendment of Division C – Section C7A - Contract Workers

Division C of the Principal Act is amended in section C7A by inserting after subsection (5) the
following new subsections —
“(6) This section shall not apply to consultants, management personnel or members of any
profession listed in the Schedule to Division C.
(7) The Minister may by Order amend the Schedule to Division C.”

(Amendment) (No. 2) Act, 2019.

Insertion of Schedule to Division C
