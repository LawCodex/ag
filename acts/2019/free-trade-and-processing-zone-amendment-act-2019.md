ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
FREE TRADE AND PROCESSING ZONE (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2019
No. 28 of 2019
AN ACT to amend the Free Trade and Processing Zone Act 1994, No. 12 of 1994 and for other
connected purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows –

---

### 1.

Short title

This Act may be cited as the Free Trade and Processing Zone (Amendment) Act 2019

### 2.

Interpretation

In this Act –
“principal Act” means the Free Trade and Processing Zone Act 1994.

### 3.

Amendment of section 12 – Licences

Section 12 of the principal Act is amended by –
(a)

deleting the word “specifically”; and

(b)

Act, 2019.

inserting after the word “law” the words “without having to obtain any permit or
other licences in respect thereof.”

Repeal and replacement of section 14

Section 14 of the principal Act is repealed in its entirety and is replaced as follows –
“14. Incentives
An applicant for a licence to establish and operate any industry or engage in any commercial
activity in a free trade and processing zone shall apply to the Antigua and Barbuda Investment
Authority for an incentive and concessions package as prescribed in Schedule I to the Investment
Authority Act 2006.”
