No. 22 of 2019

AN ACT to amend the Seventh-Day Adventist Church Incorporation Act Cap 399.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

---

### 1.

Short title

This Act may be cited as the Seventh-Day Adventist Church Incorporation (Amendment) Act,
2019.

### 2.

Amendment of the Preamble to the Act

The Seventh-Day Adventist Church Incorporation Act, CAP. 399, hereinafter referred to as the
principal Act, is amended in the Preamble by repealing paragraph 1 and substituting the
following—

“Whereas there has been constituted a Conference known as the South Leeward Conference of
Seventh-day Adventists on the 1st day of May, 2019, consisting of the Churches in Antigua and
Barbuda, Montserrat, and St. Kitts and Nevis, which have been officially organized and
accepted into fellowship by vote of the delegates assembled in session, and in accordance with
the constitution of the General-Conference of Seventh-day Adventists;”

### 3.

Amendment of sections 2, 3 and 4

The principal Act is amended in sections 2, 3 and 4 by deleting the words “South Leeward
Mission of Seventh-day Adventists.” wherever they appear and substituting the words “South
Leeward Conference of Seventh-day Adventists.”.
