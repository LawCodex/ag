STATUTORY INSTRUMENT
2019, NO. 65
DECLARATION dated 20th December, 2019, made by the Secretary to the Cabinet pursuant to
section 3 of the Land Acquisition Act Cap. 233 for the acquisition of the parcel of land located at
Friars Hill Road in the Parish of St. John’s and described in the Schedule hereto for a public
purpose.
IT IS HEREBY DECLARED that the Cabinet at its meeting held on Wednesday 10th July,
2019 considered that a portion of the parcel of land situate at Friars Hill Road in the Parish of St.
John’s and described in the Schedule hereto be acquired for a public purpose, namely, to facilitate
the construction and expansion of the Public Highway more commonly known as Friars Hill Road.
