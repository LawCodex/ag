TAX ADMINISTRATION AND PROCEDURE (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2019
No. 34 of 2019

AN ACT to amend the Tax Administration and Procedure Act and other matters connected
therewith
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:
Short title
This Act may be cited as the Tax Administration and Procedure (Amendment) Act, 2019
Interpretation
In this Act—
“principal Act” means the Tax Administration and Procedure Act, 2018
Amendment of section 2 - Interpretation
The principal Act is amended in subsection (1) by the deletion of the definition of the word
“person” and the substitution therefor of the following“person” includes a corporation, partnership, individual, estate, trust or any entity corporate or
incorporate

Tax Administration and Procedure (Amendment) Act, 2019.

Repeal and replacement of section 25-Information returns
Section 25 of the principal Act is repealed and replaced by the following–
“25. Tax returns
The provisions of this Act relating to tax returns apply to a person required by a law to which this
Act applies to file a tax return in respect of the business or trade of the person.”
Amendment of section 37 – Appeal from administrative review
Section 37 of the principal Act is amended as followsby the repeal of subsection (1) and the substitution of the following“(1) Subject to this section, a person aggrieved by the decision of the Commissioner
under section 36, may appeal within 90 days to the Tax Appeal Board constituted under
section38, and
by the repeal of subsection (2) paragraph (b) and the substitution of the following“(b) ninety days have elapsed since the request for administrative review and no decision
has been received from the Commissioner”
Amendment of Schedule
Schedule 1 of the principal Act is amended by inserting after “Travel Tax” thereof of the
following“Laws to which responsibility for the general administration of the tax is assigned to the
Commissioner.
Entertainment Duty Act, Cap.154
Telecommunication Act, Cap.423
Board of Education Act, 1994 No.11 of 1994
Gambling Act, 2016 No.22 of 2016
