ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
SPECIAL ECONOMIC ZONE (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2019
No. 27 of 2019
AN ACT to amend the Special Economic Zone Act 2015 and for other connected purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows –
1.

Short title

This Act may be cited as the Special Economic Zone (Amendment) Act 2019
2.

Interpretation

In this Act –
“principal Act” means the Special Economic Zone Act 2015.
3.

Amendment of section 5 – Purpose of Special Economic Zones

Section 5 of the priuncipal Act is amended in subsection (1) by deleting all the words after “using”
and replacing these with the following words –
“targeted support measures to encourage and facilitate the development of and investment in
manufacturing, technology and tradable services.”

4.

Amendment of section 21 – Support measures

Section 21 of the principal Act is repealed in its entirety and replaced as follows –
(1) The Minister may, in consultation with the Minister of any other relevant government
Ministry and the Advisory Board, design and adminster support measures, other than
measures having the effect as an incentive scheme, for businesses operating within a
Special Economic Zone.
(2) The Antigua and Barbuda Investment Authority may, in consultation with the Advisory
Board, and in keeping with the criteria prescribed in Schedule I to the Investment
Authority Act 2006, design, develop a nd offer incentive schemes aimed at –
(a) attracting investments to any planned Special Economic Zone;
(b) supporting the development and operation of different categories of Special
Economic Zones.
5.

Amendment of section 23 – Application for designation

Section 23 of the principal Act is amended by inserting after subsection (3) the following new
subsection as follows –
“(3A) If the applicant is a company registered under the International Business Corporation
Act, Cap. 222, the applicant must attach to the application a duplicate copy of the certificate
to conduct business locally, within Antigua and Barbuda, issued by the Financial Services
Regulatory Commission.”
6.

Amendment of section 33 – Special Economic Zone operator permit

Section 33 of the principal Act is amended as follows –
(a)

by deleting the word “and” appearing at the end of paragraph (c); then by deleting
the words “permit.” and replacing these with the words, “permit; and”

(b)

by inserting a new paragraph (e) to read as follows –
“(e) a copy of the certificate from the Antigua and Barbuda Investment Authority as
evidence that the operator satisfies the investment criteria specified in Schedule I of
the Antigua and Barbuda Investment Authority Act.”
