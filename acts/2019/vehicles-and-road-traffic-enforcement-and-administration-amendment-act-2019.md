THE VEHICLES AND ROAD TRAFFIC (ENFORCEMENT AND ADMINISTRATION)
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2019
No. 14 of 2019
AN ACT to amend the Vehicles and Road Traffic (Enforcement and Administration) Act Cap
461.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:
1.

Short title

This Act may be cited as the Vehicles and Road Traffic (Enforcement and Administration)
(Amendment) Act, 2019.
2.

Interpretation

In this Act—
“principal Act” means the Vehicles and Road Traffic (Enforcement and Administration ) Act,
Cap 461.

Amendment of section 2-Interpretation

The principal Act is amended in section 2 by inserting the following definition —

“Minister” means the Minister with responsibility for Public Safety;

Amendment of section 11-Amendment of Schedule

The principal Act is amended in section 11 by—
(a)

repealing the word “Governor-General” and substituting the word “Minister”;

(b)

repealing subsection (1) (b) and substituting the following—
“(b) remove any offence from the Schedule, and in particular he may alter
the fixed penalty for any offence or prescribe a new fixed penalty in an
amount not exceeding five hundred dollars.”.

### 5.

Amendment of section 12-Regulations

The principal Act is amended in section 12 by repealing the word “Governor-General” and
substituting the word “Minister”.
