ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Law Revision (Amendment)
Act, 2005.

Short title.

### 2.

The Law Revision Act is amended by the insertion, immediately after Section 16, of the following section:

Insertion of Section 16A Cap. 245.

“Formal
rectification of
written law by
AttorneyGeneral.

The Attorney-General may, at any time, by
order published in the Gazette rectify any
written law so as —
(a)

to alter the order of the provisions
in the written law and, in all cases
where it is necessary to do so, to
renumber the provisions of that
law;

(b)

to alter the form or arrangement
of any provision of the written
