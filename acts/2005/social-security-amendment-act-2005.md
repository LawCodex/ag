No. 5 of 2005
AN ACT to amend the Social Security Act, Cap. 408.
[ Published in the Official Gazette Vol. XXV No.35
dated 2nd June, 2005. ]
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as
follows —

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Social Security (Amendment)
Act, 2005.

Short title.

### 2.

The Social Security Act, Cap. 408 is amended in section 54
as follows —

Amendment of
section 54.

(a)

in section (1), by the deletion of the words:
“equivalent to the minimum rate paid as age benefit
under the Act” and the substitution of the words:
“at a rate to be determined by the Minister by
regulation published in the Gazette”.

(b)

by the repeal of subsection (2) and the substitution
of the following —
“(2) Notwithstanding subsection (1), any
person who, on the coming into force of this
Act is in receipt of old age assistance because
he —
