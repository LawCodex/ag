No. 4 of 2005
AN ACT to amend the Pensions (Non-Established Government
Employees) Act.
[ Published in the Official Gazette Vol. XXV No.35
dated 2nd June, 2005. ]
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as
follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Pension (Non-Established
Government Employees) (Amendment) Act, 2005 and shall be
deemed to have come into operation on the 1st January, 2005.

Short title and
commencement.

### 2.

The Pension (Non-Established Government Employees)
Act is amended by the addition, immediately after section 7, of
the following section:

Addition of
section 7A
Cap. 310.

“Minimum
amount of
pension

7A. (1)Notwithstanding sections 6 and 7,
where the amount of pension calculated at the
rate prescribed in section 6 or 7 is less than $750
per month, a non-established employee shall be
eligible to a pension at the rate of $750 per month
in lieu of the amount calculated at the rate
prescribed in any of those sections.
(2) Subsection (1) shall also apply to a
person who, on the 1st January, 2005, was in
receipt of a pension.
