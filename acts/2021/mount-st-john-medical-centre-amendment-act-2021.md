ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
THE MOUNT ST. JOHN’S MEDICAL CENTRE (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2021
No. 11 of 2021
AN ACT to amend the Mount St. John’s Medical Centre Act 2009, No. 2 of 2009 and for other
connected purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Mount St. John’s Medical Centre (Amendment) Act 2020.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act, “principal Act” means the Mount St. John’s Medical Centre Act 2009, No. 2 of 2009.

### 3.

Amendment of section 10
Section 10 of the principal Act is amended by –
(a)

deleting the words, “and daily management of the Hospital” from the subject heading;

(b)

deleting the number “(1)” before subsection (1); and

(c)

repealing subsection (2) in its entirety;

Passed the House of Representatives on
the 25th day of May, 2021.

Passed the Senate on the 3rd day of
June, 2021.
