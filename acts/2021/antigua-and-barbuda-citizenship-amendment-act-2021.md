ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA CITIZENSHIP (AMENDMENT) ACT 2021
No. 5 of 2021

An ACT to amend the Antigua and Barbuda Citizenship Act, Cap. 22
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows—

---

### 1.

Short Title

This Act may be cited as Antigua and Barbuda Citizenship (Amendment) Act, 2021.

### 2.

Amendment of section 2 - Interpretation

The Antigua and Barbuda Citizenship Act, Cap. 22 is amended in section 2 subsection (8) thereof by
repealing paragraph (a) and replacing this with the following:
“(a)

of any period or periods amounting in total to six months or more, during which a
person was not lawfully resident in Antigua and Barbuda;”
