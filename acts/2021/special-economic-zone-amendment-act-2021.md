ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
SPECIAL ECONOMIC ZONE (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2021
No. 17 of 2021

AN ACT to amend the Special Economic Zone Act 2015 to redefine the meaning of Special
Economic Zone and for other connected purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

---

### 1.

Short title

This Act may be cited as the Special Economic Zone (Amendment) Act, 2021.

### 2.

Interpretation

In this Act—
“principal Act” means the Special Economic Zone Act, 2015 No. 17 of 2015.

### 3.

Amendment of section 2-Interpretation

The principal Act is amended in section 2 by the repeal of the definition of “Special Eonomic Zone
and the substitution therefor of the following-

“Special Economic Zone” means a multifunctional zone of approximately 500 acres, or any lesser
acreage as may be determined by the Cabinet, that leverages domestic and foreign direct investment
in value added and export oriented manufacturing industries re-export, finance and services”.
