ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
THE INTERPRETATION (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2021
No. 26 of 2021

AN ACT to amend the Interpretation Act, Cap. 224 to redefine the definition of public place and
for other connected purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

1.

Short title

This Act may be cited as the Interpretation (Amendment) Act 2021.

Interpretation

In this Act, “principal Act” means the Interpretation Act, Cap. 224.

Amendment of section 58 – Miscellaneous definitions

Section 58 of the principal Act is amended as follows:
(a)

in the definition of “public place” by repealing the definition and replacing it as
follows – “public place” includes any public highway, street, road, square, court,
Parliament building, place of worship, alley, land, bridleway, footpath, parade,
wharf, jetty, quay, bridge, public garden or open square, and every threatre, place of
public entertainment of any kind, or other place of general resort, admission to
which is obtained with or without payment, or to which the public have access;”

(b)

in the definition of “signature” by repealing the definition and replacing it as follows
– “signature” and “signed” include and apply to the making of a mark or to any
authorized electronic signature.”
