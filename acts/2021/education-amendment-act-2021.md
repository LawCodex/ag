EDUCATION (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2021
No. 24 of 2021

AN ACT to amend the Education Act, No. 21 of 2008.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

---

### 1.

Short title

This Act may be cited as the Education (Amendment) Act, 2021.

### 2.

Interpretation

In this Act –
“principal Act” means the Education Act, No. 21 of 2008.

### 3.

Amendment of Section 28 – Mode of admission

Section 28 subsection (1) of the principal Act is amended as follows:
(a)

in the chapeau, by deleting the word “primary”;

(b)

in paragraph (c), by inserting after the words “section 27” the words “or 30”;

(c)

in paragraph (d), by repealing paragraph (d) in its entirety and replacing it as
follows –
“(d) a certificate issued by a registered medical practitioner or the Public Health
authorities is produced indicating that the child has been vaccinated pursuant to the
recommendations of the Ministry of Health.”
