HOSPITALITY TRAINING INSTITUTE (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2021
No. 19 of 2021
AN ACT to amend the Hospitality Training Insitute Act, No. 18 of 2006.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Hospitality Training Institute (Amendment) Act, 2021.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act –
“principal Act” means the Hospitality Training Insitute Act, No. 18 of 2006.

3\. Amendment of Section 2
The principal Act is amended in section 2 by deleting the definition of Minister and substituting it
with the following new definition—
“Minister” means the Minister with responsibility for Tourism.”
