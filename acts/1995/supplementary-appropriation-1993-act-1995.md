### 2.

The sums set forth in the Schedule amounting to the sum of
Eighteen million, five hundred and ninety-seven thousand, two
hundred and eighteen dollars ($18,597,218.00)which sums have
been drawn from the Consolidated Fund and have been paid for
the services of Antigua and Barbuda during the year 1993 in
addition to the sums granted by the Appropriation Act, 1993 and
