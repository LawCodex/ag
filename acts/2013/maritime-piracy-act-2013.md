AN ACT to criminalise acts of maritime piracy.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows—

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Maritime Piracy Act 2013.

### 2.

Interpretation
(1) In this Act“piracy” means—
(a)

any illegal acts of violence or detention, or any act of depredation committed for private
ends by the crew or a passenger of a private ship or private aircraft, and directed(i)

on the high seas, against another ship or aircraft, or against persons or property
on board such ship or aircraft; or

(ii)

against a ship, aircraft, persons or property in a place outside the jurisdiction of
any State;

3\. Crime of Piracy
A person who carries out an act of piracy in the territorial waters of Antigua and Barbuda, under
this Act commits an offence and shall be liable upon conviction on indictment to imprisonment
for a term not exceeding 25 years and a fine not exceeding $500,000.00, or to both.
