### 3.

Section 15 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion aftw subsection (4) of the following subsections "(5) Notwithstanding subsection (1), a citizen of
any Member State of the Organization of the Eastern Caribbean States landing in Antigua and

the person to whom it relates.
(6) For the purposes of subsection (5) "other
recognised photo identification card" includes a
valid Driver's Licencq or Social Security card which
