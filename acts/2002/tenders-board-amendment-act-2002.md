AN ACT to amend the Tenders Board Act Cap. 424 A.
[ 6th June, 2002 ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as
follows 1. This Act may be cited as the Tenders Board (Amendment) short title
Act 2002.

### 2.

Section 4 of the Tenders Board Act, in this Act referred Amendment of section 4 of Cap. 424
to as the principal Act, is amended as follows A.

(a)

by the insertion after subsection (1) of the following -

"(2) The Board may exempt the Government or any statutory body to which this Act
applies from the tender procedure under this
Act."
(b)

by renumbering subsection (2) as subsection (3).

### 3.

Section 20 of the principal Act is amended as follows-

(a)

by the repeal of subsection (1) and
the substitutionof the following-

"(1) Whenever articles or works or any services in connection therewith are required to
be supplied or undertaken on behalf of the
Government or a statutory body to which this
Act applies, the Government or such statutory body shall apply in writing to the Board
to -

(b)

(a)

invite on its behalf tender for the
supply of those articles or for the
undertaking of the works or services in connection therewith; or

(b)

grant it exemption from the tender procedure under this Act."

by the repeal of subsection (2) and the substitution
of the following-

"(2) The application referred to in -

(a)

subsection (1) (a) shall contain
sufficient description of the articles, works or services to be supplied or undertaken;

(b)

subsection (1) (b) shall, in addition to the information provided
under paragraph (a), contain sufficient reasons for requesting the
grant of exemption from the tender procedure under this Act,"

and such application shall be signed by the
Permanent Secretary of the Ministry concerned or in the case of a statutory body by
the Managing Director or the administrative head of the statutory body as the case
may be.

by the repeal of paragraph (b) of subsection (3)

"(b) (i) invite such bodies or persons as may

(ii)

exempt the Govenunent or any statutory
body to which this Act applies from the
tender procedure under this Act if the
Board is satisfied that, having regard to
the nature and circumstances of the supply of such articles or the undertaken of
such works and services, it is expedient
and desirable so to do."

### 4.

Any exemption from the tender procedure granted by the Validation.
Board under this Act is hereby deemed to have been properly
granted.
