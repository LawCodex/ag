ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
PRISON (AMENDMENT) ACT 2017
No. 17 of 2017

AN ACT to amend the Prison Act, Cap. 341 and for incidental and connected purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows―

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Prison (Amendment) Act, 2017.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act, the principal Act means the Prison Act, CAP. 341.

### 3.

Amendment of the principal Act
The principal Act is amended in sections 3A, 4, 6, 9 and 13 by repealing the words “Governor
General” where it appears in those sections and replacing these words with the word “Minister”.

### 4.

Amendment of section 5 of the principal Act―

The principal Act is amended in section 5 by ―
(1)

repealing subsection (1) and replacing it with the following ―

“(1) The Minister may at any time by Order published in the official Gazette declare any prison
in Antigua and Barbuda to be closed; and every prison which the Minister shall so declare to be
closed shall, as from the date of the Order, cease to be used as a prison accordingly.”; and
(2) deleting the words “Governor General” where it appears in subsection (2) and replacing it with
the word “Minister”.

### 5.

Amendment of section 7 of the principal Act
Section 7 of the principal Act is amended―
in subsection (1)
(a)

by deleting the words “Governor General” and replacing these with the word
“Minister”; and

(b)

by deleting the word “he” appearing between the words “as” and “may” and
replacing it with the words “the Cabinet”.

(1) In subsection (2) by deleting the words “Governor General” and replacing these with the word
“Minister”.

### 6.

Amendment of section 7A of the principal Act
Section 7A is amended by deleting all the words appearing between the words “Where” and “the
Minister” and replacing these with the words, “a contract has been entered into as provided under
section 3A(1),” .

### 7.

Repeal of section 11 of the principal Act
The principal Act is amended by repealing section 11 in its entirety.

### 8.

Amendment of section 26 of the principal Act
Section 26 of the principal Act is amended by inserting after subsection (2) thereof a new subsection
(3) to read as follows ―
“(3) All references to the Governor General in the Prison Rules made under the authority of this
section shall be construed as a reference to the Minister.”
