No. 7 of 2017

An Act to amend the Friendly Societies Act, Cap. 184
Enacted by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

---

### 1.

Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Friendly Societies (Amendment) Act, 2017.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act “principal Act” means the Friendly Societies Act, Cap. 184.

### 3.

Amendment of section 4 of the principal Act
Section 4 of the principal Act is amended(a)

by renumbering the existing provision as subsection (1);

(b)

by adding the words “together with their addresses and occupations” after the word
“society” appearing line two of paragraph (1) (b), as renumbered;

(c)

by adding after subsection (1), the following as subsection (2):

“(2) The Registrar must also forward to the Minister a certified copy of each return sent
under section 28; together with such other documentation as may be appropriate in the long
term interests and integrity of friendly societies generally.”

### 4.

Amendment of section 26 of the principal Act
(1) Section 26 of the principal Act is amended —
(a)

by renumbering the existing provisions as subsection (1);

(b)

by inserting the words “together with their addresses and occupation” after the word
“roll” appearing in line four of subsection (1).
