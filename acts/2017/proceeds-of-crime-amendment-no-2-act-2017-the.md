THE PROCEEDS OF CRIME (AMENDMENT) (NO. 2) ACT, 2017
No. 13 of 2017
AN ACT to provide for the amendment of the Proceeds of Crime Act 1993, No. 13 of 1993 to
reform the provisions in relation to restraining orders and for related matters.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows—

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as The Proceeds of Crime (Amendment) (No. 2) Act, 2017

### 2.

Amendment of section 40 of the Proceeds of Crime Act, 1993
Section 40 of the Proceeds of Crime Act 1993, No. 13 of 1993 is repealed in its entirety and replaced
with the following —
“40. Expiry of restraining order

Where a restraining order has been made in relation to property, the restraining order shall continue
in force until —
(a)

it is discharged by the court; or

(b)

the defendant is acquitted”
