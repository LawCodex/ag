No. 19 of 2017
AN ACT to amend the Antigua and Barbuda Labour Code, Cap. 27
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

---

### 1.

Short title and commencement
This Act may be cited as the Antigua and Barbuda Labour Code (Amendment) Act, 2017 and shall
come into operation on such date as the Minister may, by Order published in the Gazette, appoint.

### 2.

Amendment of division A 6(2) of the Antigua and Barbuda Labour Code
The Antigua and Barbuda Labour Code is amended in division A 6(2)—
(a)

by inserting after paragraph (c) the following paragraph—
“(d) the Office of National Drug and Money Laundering Control Policy;”;

(b)

by renumbering paragraph “(d)” as “(e)” and paragraph “(e)” as “(f)”
respectively.
