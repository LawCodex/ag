AGREEMENT) ACT, 2017
No. 30 of 2017
AN ACT to provide for the ratification of the agreement between the Government of Antigua and
Barbuda and the Republic of France in the Caribbean Region on the delimitation of maritime space.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows —

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Delimitation of Maritime Space (Antigua and Barbuda and the Republic
of France in the Caribbean Region) (Ratification of Agreement) Act, 2017.

### 2.

Commencement
This Act shall come into effect upon publication in the Gazette.

### 3.

Ratification
The Agreement signed on the 15th March 2017 at St. John’s in Antigua and Barbuda by Honourable
Gaston Browne in his capacity as Prime Minister of Antigua and Barbuda on behalf of the
Government of Antigua and Barbuda and M. Philippe Ardanaz the Ambassador of France to the
Organisation of Eastern Caribbean States on behalf of the Republic of France is hereby ratified.

Passed by the House of Representatives on
the 10th day of October, 2017.

Passed by the Senate on the 30th
day of October, 2017.
