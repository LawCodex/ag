CIVIL SERVICE (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2017
No. 21 of 2017

An Act to amend the Civil Service Act Cap. 87.
Enacted by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

---

### 1.

Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Civil Service (Amendment) Act, 2017.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act the “principal Act” means the Civil Service Act Cap. 87.

### 3.

Amendment of section 24 of the principal Act
Section 24 of the principal Act is amended by repealing subsections (3), (4) and (5) thereof.

### 4.

Commencement
This Act shall be deemed to have come into operation on 2nd January 1985.
