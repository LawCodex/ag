ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
GAZETTE AUTHORISATION AND PUBLICATION ACT, 2017
No. 36 of 2017

An Act to provide for publication of the Antigua and Barbuda Official Gazette in printed and
electronic format and for incidental and connected purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:1. Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Gazette Authorisation and Publication Act, 2017.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act
“Gazette” includes the Official Gazette, Supplement to the Official Gazette and the
Extraordinary Gazette published by the authority of the Government in printed and in electronic
form.
“Minister” means the Minister with responsibility for Legal Affairs.

“Government Printer” means the Government Printer or any person, for the time being,
authorized by the Minister to print the Gazette.
“Editor” means the person appointed under section to be editor of the Gazette and any other
person appointed by the Public Service Commission to act in the absence of the editor.
3\. Authority to publish the Gazette
(1) The printed form of the Gazette shall continue to be published under the authority of the
Government, and published contemporaneously in electronic Portable Document Format (PDF) on
this Gazette website: www.gazette.gov.ag.
(2) The printed copy of the Gazette may be obtained at the Government Printery or at such other
places appointed by the Minister by notice published in the Gazette.
4\. Establishment of the Gazette Website
(1) For the purpose of publication of the Gazette in an electronic form, the Minister may, by Order,
establish a website of the Government where the Gazette shall be published.
(2) A publication of the Gazette at the website established by the Minister under subsection (1)
is, for all intents and purposes deemed to be publication by authority of the Government.
5\. Appointment of Editor of the Gazette
(1) For the purposes of this Act, there shall be appointed by the Public Service Commission, an
Editor of the Gazette, who shall be responsible for the preparation and management of the Gazette.
(2) The Public Service Commission shall appoint such other officers as is considered necessary
to assist the Editor in the performance of his duties.
6\. Power to authorize publication in the Gazette
(1) Where any law in force in Antigua and Barbuda requires the publication of any document,
report or a notice, such document, report or notice shall be published in the Gazette, and such
publication is, for all intent send purposes deemed to be due publication and shall be judicially
noticed.
(2) The Clerk of Parliament shall, as soon as she receives the assented copy of any law passed by
the Governor General, forward the same to the Editor of the Gazette for publication in the Gazette.
(3) The Clerk of Parliament shall forward to the Government Printer or such other printer, for the
time being appointed by the Minister, to print and publish a statutory instrument which is registered
as an instrument in the Parliamentary register of statutory instruments.
(4) No statutory instrument shall be printed and published in the Gazette unless such statutory
instrument is endorsed by the Clerk of Parliament and assigned a number and the year in which it

was made and is signed by the appropriate Minister or the person authorised by law to sign the
instrument and assigned a serial number by the Clerk of Parliament.
(5) Despite the provisions of this section, the Minister, on the advice of the Cabinet, publish any
document, papers or such other written material which, in the opinion of the Cabinet, is in the public
interest.
7\. Publication of consolidated indexes of Acts, Statutory instruments, documents and other
papers
There shall be prepared by the Clerk to Parliament annual indexes of Acts and Statutory instruments
in force at any time after the end of the proceeding calendar year, which shall be published in the
Gazette, not later than the 7th of January of each year.
8\. Status of matters published in the Gazette
(1) Every copy of the Gazette, published by authority on the Gazette website: www.
gazette.gov.ag or printed by the Government Printer or any other printer authorized by the Minister,
shall be judicially noticed and admissible in evidence by every court and every legal proceedings
without any proof being given that the copy was so published until the contrary is shown.
(2) Every copy of the Gazette, published by authority on the Gazette website www.
Gazette.gov.ag. or printed by the Government Printer or any other printer authorized by the Minister,
is evidence of the contents contained in such Gazette as prima facie evidence of any appointment,
notification, or publication.

9\. Offences and penalties
(1) Any person who—
(a)

prints any paper which falsely purports to be a duly authorized copy of the Gazette;

(b)

wilfully causes to be published in the Gazette any false or inaccurate matter; or

(c)

tenders in evidence any such paper falsely purporting to be the Gazette, knowing that
the same was not duly printed and published, commits an offence and is liable on
summary conviction to a fine of ten thousand dollars to imprisonment for a term not
exceeding for six months.

10\. Regulations
The Minister may by regulation—

(a)

determine the manner, form, times and occasions in which the Gazette is published

(b)

prescribe the fees and charges for the subscription of the Gazette.

(c)

prescribe the charges for publication of notices, advertisement and other documents in
the Gazette

11\. Date of Commencement
This Act shall come into force on such date as the Minister may, by Notice, publish in the Gazette.
12\. Repeal of Cap. 187
The Gazette (Authorization) Act, Cap 187 is hereby repealed.
