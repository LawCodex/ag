THE EVIDENCE (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2017
No. 15 of 2017
AN ACT to amend The Evidence (Special Provisions) Act 2009 No. 5 of 2009 in order to confer
on officers of the Office of National Drug and Money Laundering Control Policy certain powers
already exercisable by the police under the Act.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows1. Short Title
This Act may be cited as The Evidence (Special Provisions) (Amendment) Act, 2017.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act, the “principal Act” means The Evidence (Special Provisions) Act 2009, No. 5 of 2009

The Evidence (Special Provisions) (Amendment) Act, 2017.

3\. Amendment of section 57 of the principal Act
Section 57 of the principal Act is amended in subsection (9) by inserting after the words “police
officer” the words “or an officer of the ONDCP”.
4\. Amendment of section 60 the principal Act
Section 60 of the principal Act is amended in subsection (2) by inserting after the words “police
officer” the words “or an officer of the ONDCP”.
