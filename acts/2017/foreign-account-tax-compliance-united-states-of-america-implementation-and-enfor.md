FOREIGN ACCOUNT TAX COMPLIANCE ACT (UNITED STATES OF
AMERICA) (IMPLEMENTATION AND ENFORCEMENT OF INTERGOVERNMENTAL AGREEMENT) ACT, 2017
No. 2 of 2017
AN ACT to make provision for authorising the enforcement of the obligations of Antigua and Barbuda
under an inter-governmental agreement for cooperation to facilitate the implementation of the Foreign
Account Tax Compliance Act (United States of America).

ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

---

### 1.

Short title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act (United States of
America) (Implementation and Enforcement) Act, 2017.
(2) This Act comes into operation at 11:59pm on the 31 st December, 2016

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires:
“Agreement” means the Inter-governmental Agreement between the Government of Antigua
and Barbuda and Government of The United States of America to improve international Tax
compliance and to implement FATCA, as set out in the Schedule;
“Competent Authority” means the Commissioner of Inland Revenue;
“Contracting parties” means the parties to the Agreement as set out in the Schedule;
“Financial institution” means a reporting financial institution or a person who carries on
business in Antigua and Barbuda as:
(a)

a custodial institution;

(b)

“Court” means the Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court;

(c)

“FATCA” means the Act commonly known as the Foreign Account Tax
Compliance Act enactment in the United States called the Hiring Incentives to
Restore Employment Act.

(d)

“reportable account” means reportable account as set out in the Schedule.

### 3.

Articles of Agreement to have the force of law
The Articles of the Agreement shall have the force of law in Antigua and Barbuda.

### 4.

Amendment of Agreement
Where an amendment to the Agreement is accepted by both the Government of Antigua and Barbuda
and the Government of the United States of America, the Minister of Finance may amend the
Schedule for the purpose of including the amendments.

### 5.

Competent Authority to exercise powers and authorities
(1) The Commissioner of Inland Revenue is hereby designated the Competent Authority.

(2) The Competent Authority shall exercise his or her functions acting alone, or through a person
designated by the Competent Authority to Act on his behalf in an administrative capacity.
(3) The Competent Authority may exercise all powers vested in him hereunder—
(a)

to administer and process any request made under the Agreement;

(b)

for the rendering of reciprocal assistance to facilitate the administration of the
Agreement.

(4) Without prejudice to the generality of subsection (1), the Competent Authority may request a
financial institution to furnish to him at a time and in manner as the Competent Authority may
determine information and data as he may require for the proper discharge of his functions and
responsibilities under the Agreement.
(5) Notwithstanding, any other law relating to confidentiality or secrecy of the Laws of Antigua
and Barbuda, a financial institution may communicate or allow to be communicated any information
required to be disclosed to or by the Competent Authority under the Agreement except the same is
prohibited by an order of a court of competent jurisdiction in Antigua and Barbuda.
(6) A financial institution which refuses to or fails, without lawful excuse, to provide any
information requested under this section commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction
to fine of Twenty-seven Thousand Dollars.
(7) A financial institution is liable to penalty of Twenty-seven Thousand Dollars if, without
lawful excuse, it fails to provide accurate information, as requested under subsection (4).)

### 6.

Compliance officer to identify reportable accounts
(1) In relation to all financial institutions, the financial institution shall appoint a Compliance
Officer who shall be responsible for maintaining arrangements that are designed to identify
reportable accounts
(2) The financial institution shall be taken to have complied with this obligation only if the
arrangements meet the due diligence requirements set out in the Schedule of the Agreement.

### 7.

Confidentiality
(1) Subject to this section, the Competent Authority and any person employed in carrying out the
provisions of or having any official duty under the Agreement shall regard and deal with as
confidential-

(a)

all documents and information relation to any person, and

(b)

all confidential information

With respect to the administration of the Agreement, which may come into his possession or to his
knowledge in the course of the performance of his duties
(2) The obligation as to confidentially imposed by this section continues to apply to a person
despite him having ceased to be appointed under or employed in carrying out the provisions of the
Agreement.
(3) A person who discloses or divulges any information or produces any document in
contravention of this section commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to fine of
Thirteen Thousand Five Hundred Dollars or One year imprisonment.
(4) Nothing in this section applies to the disclosure of any confidential information to a person to
whom disclosure is necessary for the purposes of the Agreement.

### 8.

Restrictions on use of information provided
The Competent Authority shall not consent to the use or transmission by the Government of the
United States of America of information provided under this Act or Agreement for purposes,
investigations or proceedings other than those stated in the Agreement, unless the transmission or
use is authorised by the Court.

### 9.

Costs
The Competent Authorities of the Contracting Parties to the Agreement may agree on the incidence
of costs arising out of a request for the exchange of information

### 10.

Immunity from suit
(1) The Competent Authority or a person acting under his authority who discloses confidential
information in compliance with section 5 does not commit an offence under any other confidentiality
or secrecy of the laws of Antigua and Barbuda by reason of such the disclosure.
(2) A disclosure referred to in subsection (1) is not a breach of a confidential relationship between
the person who discloses the information and any other person, and no civil claim or action
whatsoever lies against the person making the disclosure by reason of the disclosure

### 11.

Regulations
(1) The Minister of Finance may make Regulations to give effect to the provisions of this Act and
the Agreement.
(2) The Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act (United States of America) (Implementation and
Enforcement of Inter-governmental Agreement) (Antigua and Barbuda) Regulations 2017 are
deemed to have been passed pursuant to this section.
