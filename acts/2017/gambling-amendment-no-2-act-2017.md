AN ACT to amend the Gambling Act, 2016 No. 22 of 2016
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows

---

### 1.

Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Gambling (Amendment) (No. 2) Act, 2017.

### 2.

Amendment of sections 394,395,397,398,400,402 and 403 of the Gambling Act, 2016.
Sections 394,395,397,398,400,402 and 403 of the Gambling Act, 2016 are amended by the
deletion of word “Authority” wherever it occurs and the substitution therefor of the words
“Commissioner of Inland Revenue”.
