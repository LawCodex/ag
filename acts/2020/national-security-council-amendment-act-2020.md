THE NATIONAL SECURITY COUNCIL (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2020
No. 19 of 2020

AN ACT to amend the National Security Council Act 2006, No. 14 of 2006 and for other
connected purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

---

### 1.

Short title

This Act may be cited as the National Security Council (Amendment) Act 2020.

### 2.

Interpretation

In this Act, “principal Act” means the National Security Council Act 2006, No. 14 of 2006.

### 3.

Amendment of section 4 – Composition

Section 4 subsection (1) of the principal Act is amended by inserting after paragraph (f) the
following new paragraphs –

The National Security Council (Amendment) Act, 2020.

“(fa) Chief Immigration Officer;
(fb) Comptroller of Customs”
