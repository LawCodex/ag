NO. 33 OF 2020

AN ACT to amend the Immigration and Passport Act, 2014
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows —

---

### 1.

Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Immigration and Passport (Amendment) (No. 3) Act 2020.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act, “the principal Act” means the Immigration and Passport Act, 2014

### 3.

Amendment of section 40 – Grant of residence permit
The principal Act is amended in section 40 as follows –
(a)

In subsection (1)
(i)

(A)

at paragraph (c)

by deleting the word “for” and replacing this with the word “from”; and

No. 33 of 2020.

(B)

(No. 3) Act, 2020

by deleting the words “married to” and replacing this with the words, “in a marital
union with”.
(ii)

by inserting after paragraph (c) a new paragraph as follows –

“(d) In this Part, “marital union” means that the applicant and his or her Antigua
and Barbuda citizen spouse reside together in the same household in a marital
relationship”.
(b)

### 4.

In subsection (7) by inserting immediately after “Section 39” the words “and 47”

Amendment of section 41 – Refusal of grant of residence permit

Section 41 subsection (1) of the principal Act is amended in paragraph (a) by inserting after the
words “section 39” the words “or 47”

### 5.

Amendment of section 47 - Residence permit for spouses, etc. of citizens of Antigua and
Barbuda

Section 47 of the principal Act is amended in subsection (3) by inserting after paragraph (a)
thereof the following new paragraph—
“(aa) the spouse and the applicant are in a marital union;”

### 6.

Amendment of section 48 – Loss of right of residence

Section 48 subsection (1) of the principle Act is amended in paragraph (e) thereof by deleting all
the words appearing after “living apart” and replacing these with the words “or whatever reason are
no longer in a marital union.”
