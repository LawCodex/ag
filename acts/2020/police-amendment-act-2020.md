ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
THE POLICE (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2020
No. 34 of 2020
AN ACT to amend the The Police Act, Cap. 330 to increase the pensionable age for members of
the Royal Police Force of Antigua and Barbuda and for other connected purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

---

### 1.

Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Police (Amendment) Act, 2020.

### 2.

Interpretation

In this Act—
“principal Act” means the Police Act, Cap. 330

### 3.

Amendment of section 68
The principal Act is amended by repealing section 68 and replacing it with the following new
section 68 “68. Retirement from the Force
(1) Every Inspector, subordinate police officer and constable shall retire from the Force on
attaining the age of sixty-five years.

No. 34 of 2020.

(2) Every Inspector, subordinate police officer or constable may be required or permitted to
retire from the Force –
(a)

on or after attaining the age of sixty years if the member opts to retire at that age,
or on or after attaining the age fifty-five years, if the member requests to retire at
that age and the Governor General, acting on the advice of the Police Service
Commission, grants his request; or

(b)

after he or she has served in the Force for twenty years and the Governor General,
acting on the advice of the Police Service Commission grants his or her request; or

(c)

on compulsory retirement for the purpose of facilitating improvement in the
organisation of the department to which he belongs, by which greater efficiency or
economy may be effected; or

(d)

on furnishing medical evidence to the satisfaction of the Police Service Commission
that the officer is incapable by reason of infirmity of mind or body, of continuing in
such employment; or

(e)

where the retirement age of the inspector, subordinate police officer or constable is
in accordance with the corresponding pensionable age and pensionable year as set
out in the Schedule to the Social Security (Benefits) (Age Pensions and Grants)
(Amendment) Regulations, 2016.”
