STATUTORY INSTRUMENT
2020, No. 7

ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA CITIZENSHIP BY INVESTMENT (AMENDMENT)
REGULATIONS 2020 MADE BY THE MINISTER IN EXERCISE OF THE POWERS
CONFERRED UPON HIM BY SECTION 6 OF THE ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
CITIZENSHIP BY INVESTMENT ACT, 2013.
1.

Short title

These Regulastions may be cited as the Antigua and Barbuda Citizenship by Investment
(Amendment) Regulations 2020.
2.

Interpretation
(1) In these regulations –

“principal Regulations” means the Antigua and Barbuda Citizenship by Investmwent Regulations
2016.
3.

Amendment of regulation 10 Ineligible Applicants

Regulation 10 of the principal Regulations is amended by repealing paragraph (b) thereof and
replacing it as follows –
“(b) having not received a free pardon, was at any time convicted of criminal offence and –
(i)

was sentenced to serve a period of imprisonment in excess of twelve (12)
months; and

(ii)

the offence and conviction is such that had it occurred within Antigua and
Barbuda, the applicant would be incapable of being regarded as a

rehabilitated person under the provisions of the Criminal records
(Rehabilitation of Offenders) Act, 2013”
