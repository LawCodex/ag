AN ACT to amend the Antigua and Barbuda Labour Code, Cap. 27
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

---

### 1.

Short title

This Act may be cited as the Antigua and Barbuda Labour Code (Amendment) Act 2020

### 2.

Amendment of Division F, Section F5 – Application for work permit

The Antigua and Barbuda Labour Code is amended in section F5 of Division F as follows —

(a)

in subsection (1) by deleting the words, “complete in triplicate;”;

(b)

in subsection (2) be repealing the words “ten dollars” and substituting therefor the
words “one hundred dollars”.
