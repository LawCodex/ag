No. 5 of 2020

AN ACT to amend the Immigration and Passport Act, 2014
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows —

---

### 1.

Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Immigration and Passport (Amendment) Act 2020.

### 2.

Interpretation

In this Act, “the principal Act” means the Immigration and Passport Act, 2014

### 3.

Amendment of Section 51

Section 51 of the principal Act is hereby amended in subsection (1) (a) by the deletion of the word
‘and’ appearing at the end of paragraph (a) and by substituting therefor the word ‘or’.
