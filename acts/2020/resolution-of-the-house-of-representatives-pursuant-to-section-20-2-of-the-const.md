RESOLUTION OF THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES PURSUANT TO SECTION 20
(2) OF THE CONSTITUTION OF ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA APRROVING A STATE OF
PUBLIC EMERGENCY
2020, No. 24
WHEREAS by Proclamation dated the 25th March, 2020 the Governor General declared a state
of public emergency under section 20(1) of the Constitution of Antigua and Barbuda, Cap. 23
AND WHEREAS section 20(2) of the Constitution of Antigua and Barbuda provides that –
“Every declaration shall lapse(a) in the case of a declaration made when Parliament is sitting, at the expiration of a
period of seven days beginning with the date date of publication of declaration;
and
(b) in any other case, at the expiration of a period of twenty-one days beginning with the
date of publication of the declaration, unless it has in the meantime been approved
by resolutions of both House of Parliament.”
AND WHEREAS section 20(5) provides that“A resolution of a House of Parliament passed for the purposes of his section shall remain in
force for three months or such shorter period as may be specified therein:
Provided that any such resolution may be extended from time to time by a further such
resolution each extension not exceeding three months from the due of the resolution effecting the
extension and any such resolution may be revoked at any time by a resolution of that House.”
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT this Honourable House APPROVES the
Declaration of the State of Public Emergency issued by the Governor General on the advice of the
Cabinet under section 20(1) of the Constitution of Antigua and Barbuda and published on the 25 th
March, 2020;
AND BE IT FURTHER DECLARED AND RESOLVED THAT this Honourable House
approves that the State of Public Emergency declared on the 25 th March, 2020 shall remain in
force for a period of sixty (60) days unless sooner revoked by a further Resolution of this House.
