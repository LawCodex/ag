## SCHEDULE

2020, No.13

SUPREME COURT (AMENDMENT) ORDER
STATUTORY INSTRUMENT
2020, NO. 13

In exercise of the power conferred under section 12(1) of the Supreme Court Order, Cap. 422A,
the Chief Justice with the concurrence of the Prime Ministers, Premiers and Chief Minister of all
the States and Territories, the Judical and Legal Services Commission makes the Order:
1.

Citation and commencement
(1) This Order may be cited as the Supreme Court (Amendment) Order, 2020.
(2) This Order shall be deemed to have come into force on the 1st day of September, 2019.

2.

Interpretation

In this Order “the principal Order” means The Supreme Court Order, Cap 422A of the Revised
Laws of Antigua and Barbuda 1992.
3.

Amendment of section 4

Section 4 of the principal order is amended by substituting the word “twelve” for the word “six”
appearing in subsection (3).
4.

Amendment of Schedule 3

The principal order is amended by deleting Schedule 3 and substituting the following –

“ SCHEDULE 3
(Section 11)
SALARIES OF JUDGES OF SUPREME COURT

Chief Justice
Court of Appeal Judge
High Court Judge

$285,410.00 per year
$237, 838.00 per year
$198,198 per year”.

Made this 13th day of March, 2020.

DAME JANICE M. PEREIRA, DBE
Chief Justice and Chairperson of
the Judicial and Legal Services
Commission
