TRANSPORT BOARD (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2020
No. 28 of 2020
AN ACT to amend the Transport Board Act 1995, No. 13 of 1995 to increase the membership of
the Board.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

---

### 1.

Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Transport Board (Amendment) Act, 2020.

### 2.

Interpretation

In this Act –
“principal Act” means the Transport Board Act 1995, No. 13 of 1995.

### 3.

Amendment to section 4

The principal Act is amended in section 4 by repealing subsection (1) and replacing it with the
following new subsection (1) –

Transport Board (Amendment) Act, 2020.

“(1) The Minister shall, with the approval of Cabinet, appoint the following to serve as members
of the Board –
(a)

a Chairman;

(b)

a Deputy Chairman;

(c)

the Traffic Commissioner or his nominee; and

(d)

six other members.”
