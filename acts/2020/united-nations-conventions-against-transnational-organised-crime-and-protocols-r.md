No. 20 of 2020
AN ACT to ratify the United Nations Convention Against Transnational Organized Crime And
Protocols signed on the 26th day of September 2001 at Palermo Italy by the Government of
Antigua and Barbuda.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

---

### 1.

Short Title
(1)
The Act may be cited as the United Nations Convention against Transnational Organized
Crime and Protocols (Ratification) Act, 2020.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act–
“Act” means the Mutual Legal Assistance in Criminal Matters Act, 1993, and the Money
Laundering (Prevention) Act, 1996.

“Convention” means the United Nations Convention Against Transnational Organized Crime
And Protocols 2000 signed by the Government of Antigua and Barbuda on the 26th day of
September 2001 at Palermo Italy, the text of which is set out in the Schedule.
“Minister” means the Minister of Legal Affairs

### 3.

Ratification of United Nations Convention Against Transnational Organised Crime and
Protocols

The United Nations Convention Against Transnational Organized Crime And Protocols which was
signed by the Government of Antigua and Barbuda on the 26th day of September 2001 at Palermo
Italy is hereby ratified in accordance with section 3 (3) of the Ratification of Treaties Act, Cap 364
and is deemed to have entered into force with respect to Antigua and Barbuda on the 1st day of
September 2020.[ or on a day appointed by the Minister and published in the Gazette]

### 4.

Application of the Act.

The procedures prescribed in the Act shall apply to the execution of any request made by the
competent authority of any country whether or not that country is a Commonwealth country.
