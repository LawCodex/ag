PUBLIC HEALTH ACT (DECLARATION OF A DANGEROUS INFECTIOUS DISEASE)
ORDER 2020, made by a Minister pursuant to Section 100 of the Public Health Act, Cap.
353 as amended.
1.

Citation

This Order may be cited as the Public Health Act (Declaration of a Dangerous Infectious Disease)
Order, 2020.
2.

Declaration of Covid-19 as a Dangerous Infectious Disease

The Minister of Health, pursuant to section 100 of the Public Health Act, Cap. 353, as amended
hereby declare the new coronavirus officially named Covid-19 to be a dangerous infectious
disease.

Made the 20th day of March, 2020.

Hon. Molwyn Joseph,
Minister of Health.
