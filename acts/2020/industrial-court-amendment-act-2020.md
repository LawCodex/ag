No. 2 of 2020

AN ACT to amend the Industrial Court Act CAP. 214.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

---

### 1.

Short title

This Act may be cited as The Industrial Court (Amendment) Act, 2020.

### 2.

Interpretation

In this Act—
“principal Act” means the Industrial Court Act, CAP, 214.

### 3.

Amendment of section 4- Establishment of Industrial Court

The principal Act is amended in section 4—
(1) by repealing paragraph “a” of subsection (4) and substituting the following paragraph—
“(a) a President and Vice President, each being—“
(2) by inserting in subsection (5) after the word “President” wherever it appears the words
“or Vice President” .

No. 2 of 2020.

(3) by inserting in subsection (8) after the word “President” the words “or Vice President”.

### 4.

Amendment of section 5-Period of Appointment, remuneration etc

The principal Act is amended in section 5 by inserting after the word “President” a “comma” and
the words “Vice President”.

### 5.

Amendment of section 7-Jurisdiction of Court

The principal Act is amended in section 7 subsection (5) by inserting after the word “President”
the words “or Vice President”.

### 6.

Amendment of section 25-Awards

The principal Act is amended in section 25 subsection (2) by inserting after the word “President”
wherever it appears the words “or Vice President”.
