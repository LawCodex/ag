AN ACT to amend the Stem Cell Research and Therapy Act, 2019 No. 11 of 2019.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

---

### 1.

Short title

This Act may be cited as the Stem Cell Research and Therapy (Amendment) Bill 2020

### 2.

Interpretation

In this Act—
“principal Act” means the Stem Cell Research and Therapy Act 2019 No. 11 of 2020

### 3.

Amendment of section 4 – Establishment of the Stem Cell Oversight Committee

Section 4 of the principal Act is amended –

No. 4 of 2020.

Stem Cell Research and Therapy
(Amendment) Act, 2020.

(a) in subsection (1) by repealing the word “five” and replacing it with the word “seven”; and
(b) in subsection (7) by repealing the word “Four” and replacing it with the word “Five”

### 4.

Amendment of section 5 – Functions of the National Stem Cell Oversight Committee

Section 5 of the principal Act is amended by relettering the current paragraph (h) as paragraph (i)
and by inserting immediately after paragraph (g) a new paragraph (h) as follows –
“(h) issue guidelines;”
