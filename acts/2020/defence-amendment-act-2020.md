ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
DEFENCE (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2020
No. 18 of 2020

AN ACT to amend the Defence Act 2006 to make provision for the unificationof the duties of the
officers and soldiers of the Antigua and Barbuda Defence Force and for other connected purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

---

### 1.

Short title

This Act may be cited as the Defence (Amendment) Act 2020.

Interpretation

In this Act, “principal Act” means the Defence Act 2006.
