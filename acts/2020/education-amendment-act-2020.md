ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
THE EDUCATION (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2020
No. 21 of 2020
AN ACT to amend the Education Act, 2008, No. 21 of 2008 and for incidental and connected
purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Education (Amendment) Act, 2020.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act “the principal Act” means the Education Act 2008, No. 21 of 2008.

### 3.

Amendment of section 81(3(a) and 81(3) (b) of principal Act
Paragraphs (a) and (b) of subsection (3) of Section 81 of the principal Act is amended by repealing
paragraphs (a) and (b) of subsection (3) of Section 81 and substituting therefor the following—
“81(3)(a) The initial educational plan shall be prepared and provided to the Director of
Education three months or such shorter period as the Director deems necessary, prior to the
commencement of the home education programme;

No. 21of 2020
(b)

The educational plan shall be for a minimum period of one school year or such
shorter period as the Director deems necessary, and shall cover every year of the
home education programme;”
