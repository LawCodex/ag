THE GUARANTEE OF LOANS (REPEAL) ACT, 2007
No. 14 of 2007
AN ACT to repeal The Guarantee of Loans Act
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Guarantee of Loans (Repeal) Act, 2007.
2\. Repeal
The Guarantee of Loans Act, Cap. 196 is repealed.
