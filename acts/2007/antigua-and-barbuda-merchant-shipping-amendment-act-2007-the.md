ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
THE ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA MERCHANT SHIPPING
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2007
NO. 12 OF 2007
AN ACT to amend the Antigua and Barbuda Merchant Shipping Act, 2006, so as to permit the
Antigua and Barbuda Department of Marine Services and Merchant Shipping to establish offices
outside of Antigua and Barbuda, to allow registration for ships under construction, to establish registration fees and criteria for the refund of fees, to extend to Antigua and Barbuda the provisions of
Annex VI of the International Convention to Prevent Pollution from Ships, to revise the provisions
relating to births and deaths at sea and for incidental and connected purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Antigua and Barbuda Merchant Shipping (Amendment) Act, 2007.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act, the “principal Act” means the Antigua and Barbuda Merchant Shipping Act, 2006.
3\. New section 6A
The principal Act is amended by inserting after section 6 the following section —
“6A.

Additional offices of ADOMS

(1) The Minister may establish additional offices of ADOMS outside Antigua and Barbuda.

(2) The Director shall, on the direction of the Minister, assign the duties to the additional offices
that the Minister directs.”
4\. New section 11A
The principal Act is amended by inserting after section 11 the following section —
“11A. Ships under construction
A ship that is under construction whose keel has been laid and which is wholly owned by
persons specified in subsection 11(1) may be registered as a ship under construction, and sections 16 to 19 shall not apply to such a ship until construction is finished.”
5\. Amendment to section 13
The principal Act is amended by replacing paragraph 13(1)(b) with the following —
“(b) a register for yachts;”
6\. Amendment to section 23
The principal Act is amended in section 23—
(a)

in subsection 23(2) by deleting the word “registration” and substituting the word “registry” after the words “certificate of”;

(b)

in subsection 23(3) by deleting the word “tithe” and substituting the word “title”.

7\. New Sections 26A and 26B
The principal Act is amended by inserting after section 26 the following sections—
“26A. Fees
(1) The owner of an Antigua and Barbuda ship shall pay the prescribed registration fee in respect
of a ship upon its registration.
(2) The owner of an Antigua and Barbuda ship registered permanently shall pay, every year on the
anniversary date of its initial registration, the prescribed annual fee in respect of that ship for that
year.
(3) The owner of an Antigua and Barbuda ship registered for bareboat charter shall pay, every two
years on the anniversary date of its initial registration, the prescribed fee in respect of that ship for that
two-year period.

(4) If the owner fails to pay the fee, the Registrar may suspend the certificate of registry of the ship.
(5) Where a ship has been re-measured and the tonnage ascertained, the new tonnage shall form
the basis of the calculation of the annual fee.
(6) The Minister may prescribe the fees to be paid under this section in respect of ships and the
fees may be fixed according to
(a)

their tonnage;

(b)

whether they are to be registered permanently or for bareboat charter; and

(c)

any other relevant criteria.

26B. Refund of fees
(1) Subject to subsection (2), no fees paid under section 26A are to be refunded.
(2) Where the owner of a ship makes an application and the Director is satisfied that the registration of a ship has been transferred from permanent registration to bareboat charter registration, the
Director shall order a refund equal to the difference in the amount of fees paid for permanent registration when only bareboat registration fees were payable.”
8\. Amendments to section 115
The principal Act is amended
(a)

by deleting subparagraph 115(j)(i) and substituting the following —
“(i)

(b)

record the birth of any child born at sea and give notice or cause notice to be given
of the birth of the child to the Registrar under The Births and Deaths (Registration)
Act, Cap. 53 within thirty days from the date of birth in accordance with subsections 17(1) and (2) of that Act;”

by adding the word “and” at the end of subparagraph 115(j)(ii) and inserting a subparagraph after that subparagraph as follows —
“(iii) give or cause to be given to the registrar referred under The Births and Deaths
(Registration) Act notice in writing where death occurs at sea within thirty hours
after the death in accordance with section 18 of that Act.”

The principal Act is amended in section 133 by deleting the word ‘SOLAR’ in the marginal note and
substituting “SOLAS”.

The principal Act is amended in section 173 by repealing the numeral “V” and substituting “VI”.

### 11.

Amendment of the Second Schedule
The principal Act is amended by amending paragraph 8 of the Second Schedule in the fifth line by
deleting “ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA” and substituting “ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA”.
