### 2.

Any person who imports into Antigua and Barbuda

conviction to a fine of seven hundred and fifty dollars and
to imprisonment for three months and in addition to any

offence has been committed shall be liable to forfeiture and
disposed of in such manner as the court may determine.

### 4.

The Minister may grant a licence permitting the
importation into Antigua and Barbuda of any live fish other
than those listed in the Schedule hereto which licence may
be subject to such terms and conditions and to the payment
of such fees as the Minister may, in his absolute discretion,
determine.

### 5.

The Minister may, by Order, amend the Schedule
hereto by deleting, varying or adding to such Schedule any
species or sub-species of live fish.

### 6.

The Minister may make regulations for the better
carrying out of this Act and in particular may provide for(i)

the manner in which an application for a
licence under section 4 may be granted and
the form of such licence; and

(ii) the terms which may apply generally to any
licence granted under section 4.
