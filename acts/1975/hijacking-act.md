### 3.

Where a person of whatever nationality does on
board any aircraft wherever registered and while outside
Antigua and Barbuda any act which, if done in Antigua and
Barbuda would constitute the offence of murder, attempted
murder, manslaughter, culpable homicide or assault or an
offence under section 20,22,23,24,25,30 or 31 of the Offences
Against the Person Act, this act shall constitute that offence
if it is done in connection with the offence of hijacking committed or attempted by such person on board that aircraft.
