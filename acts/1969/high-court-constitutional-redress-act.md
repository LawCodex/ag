---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the High Court (Con- short title.
stitutional Redress) Act.

### 2.

(1) The Chief Justice may make rules with respect Rules.
to the practice and procedure of the High Court in relation
to the jurisdiction and powers conferred on that Court by
or under sections 18 and 119 of the Constitution.
(2) Rules made under subsection (1) of this section may
provide for the time within which any application under the
said sections 18 and 119 may be made.
