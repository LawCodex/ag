ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
THE STAMP (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2008
No. 7 of 2008
AN ACT to amend the Stamp Act, Cap. 410, to reduce the stamp duty payable on certain transfers of
property from a husband to a wife or a wife to a husband.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Stamp (Amendment) Act, 2008.
2\. Amendment of Schedule
The Schedule to the Stamp Act, Cap. 410, is amended
(a)

in the paragraph under the heading “CONVEYANCE or TRANSFER ON SALE”
(i)

by inserting after the words “ last preceding item and not being a conveyance or
transfer of residential property of a value of $500,000 or less to a child or grandchild of the transferor” the words “and not being a conveyance or transfer from a
husband to his wife or from a wife to her husband”; and

(ii)

by inserting after the words “lands or hereditament not being a conveyance or transfer
of residential property of a value of $500,000 or less to a child or grandchild of the

transferor” the words “and not being a conveyance or transfer from a husband to
his wife or from a wife to her husband’; and
(b)

by inserting after the paragraph dealing with conveyances to children and grandchildren
and ending with “confirm the relationship specified in paragraph (a)” the following paragraph:

“CONVEYANCE or TRANSFER of residential property to a wife by her husband or to a husband by
his wife on provision to the Commissioner of Inland Revenue of documentation that confirms the
relationship of marriage, for every $100 and any fractional part of $100, of the value of the property
conveyed or transferred……………………………………………………………..$2.00.”
Passed by the House of Representatives on
this 7th day of July, 2008.

Passed by the Senate on this 21st day of
August, 2008.
