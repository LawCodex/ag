ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
THE OFFICE OF NATIONAL DRUG AND MONEY LAUNDERING
CONTROL POLICY (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2008
No. 17 of 2008
AN ACT to amend the Office of National Drug and Money Laundering Control Policy Act.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Office of National Drug and Money Laundering Control Policy (Amendment) Act, 2008.
2\. Amendment of section 2 Interpretation
Section 2 of the Office of National Drug and Money Laundering Control Policy Act 2003, No. 11
of 2003 is amended by the repeal of paragraph (d) in the definition of “specified offence”, and the
substitution of the following—
“(d) an offence under sections 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 33 and 34 of the Prevention of Terrorism Act
2005; or”.
