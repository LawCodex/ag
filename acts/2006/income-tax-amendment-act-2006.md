ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
No. 24 of 2006
AN ACT to amend the Income Tax Act, Cap. 212, to provide for
tax credits to be available to persons who contribute substantially to sport, education or culture in Antigua and Barbuda,
and for incidental and connected purposes.
[ Published in the Official Gazette Vol. XXVI
No. 78 dated 28th December, 2006. ]
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Income Tax (Amendment) Act,
2006.

Citation.

### 2.

The Income Tax Act, Cap. 212, shall be amended by the
insertion after section 37 of the following section—

Insertion of Section 37A – Cultural
or social tax credits.

“Cultural or social tax credits
37A.The Minister of Finance may make regulations enabling credits against tax payable under this Act on gains
or profits from any trade, business or profession by persons who contribute substantially to sport, education or
culture in Antigua and Barbuda in accordance with the
provisions of such regulations.

BARBUDA

No. 24 of 2006.

(2) A tax credit under subsection (1) shall not exceed $250,000
per annum.
(3) Regulations made under subsection (1) shall prescribe—
(a)

the tax credit rate;

(b)

the procedure for claiming a tax credit;

(c)

the process to be followed by the Minister and the
Commissioner of Inland Revenue in deciding whether
to grant a tax credit that has been claimed;

(d)

the criteria that shall be fulfilled before a tax credit
may be granted; and

(e)

such other matters as the Minister may prescribe.

(4) Regulations made under subsection (1) shall be subject to
affirmative resolution of the House.”.
