# Antigua and Barbuda Merchant Shipping (Amendment) Act, 2006

BARBUDA

[ L.S.]
I Assent,
James B. Carlisle,
Governor-General.
19th September, 2006
ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
No. 9 of 2006
AN ACT to amend the Antigua and Barbuda Merchant Shipping
Act, 2006 so as to provide increased penalties for offences
under the Act, to introduce new offences for non-compliance
with the terms of the Act, to increase the time limit within
which proceedings for offences must be brought, to add new
matters on which regulations, rules and directives may be
made, to increase the minimum age at which children may be
employed as seafarers and for incidental and connected matters.

[Published in the Official Gazette Vol. XXVI No. 55
dated 28th September, 2006. ]
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Antigua and Barbuda Merchant Shipping (Amendment) Act, 2006.

Short title.

### 2.

In this Act “the principal Act” means the Antigua and
Barbuda Merchant Shipping (Amendment) Act, 2006.
