ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
THE DEFENCE (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2015
No. 15 of 2015
AN ACT to amend the Defence Act, 2006 and for incidental and connected purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Defence (Amendment) Act, 2015.
2\. Amendment of the First Schedule
The Defence Act, 2006, No 10 of 2006, is amended by repealing the First Schedule and
substituting the following—
“FIRSTSCHEDULE
(SECTION 15 (3)
FORM OF QUEEN'S COMMISSION
I..................................................... Knight/Dame Grand Cross of the Most Distinguished Order of
St. Michael and St. George, Governor-General of Antigua and Barbuda acting under command of
Her Majesty Queen Elizabeth the Second do give to .........................................................Greetings

.
and reposing especial trust in your loyalty, courage and good conduct, do by these present
constitute and appoint you to be an officer in the Antigua and Barbuda Defence Force (for
........................... years)* from the .....................day of .........................................................
20..................
You are therefore carefully and diligently to discharge your duty as such an officer in the rank of
................................................................................ or in such other rank as you may from time to
time hereafter be promoted or appointed and you are in such manner and on such occasion to
exercise and well discipline in their duties such officers and soldiers as may be placed under your
orders from time to time and use your best endeavours to keep them in good order and discipline. I
do hereby command all such officers and soldiers to obey you as their superior officer, and you to
observe and follow such orders and directions as from time to time you shall receive from me or
any of your superior officers in pursuance of the trust hereby reposed in you.
Given under my hand and the Broad Seal of Antigua and Barbuda at………………………………
