AN ACT to amend the Firearm Act, Cap 171
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows––

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Firearms (Amendment) Act, 2015.

### 2.

Amendment of section 15A-Firearms licensing committee
(1) Subsection (1) of section 15A of the Firearms Act Cap. 171, as amended, is repealed and substituted by the following––
“15 A (1) There is hereby established a Firearms Licensing Committee, in this Act, referred to as
‘The Committee’, consisting of the following––
(a)

the Commissioner of Police, or in his absence, the Deputy Commissioner of Police, who
shall be the Chairperson; and;

(b)

two other persons of recognised standing and experience in the field of defence and national security to be appointed by the Minister.”

3\. Insertion of subsection (3)
There is hereby inserted after subsection (2) of section 15A, the following new subsections“(3) Where a situation arises which requires the grant of a licence to any person to carry a firearm,
and the Committee, is for any reason, unable to meet and consider the application, the Commissioner
may, subject to the provisions of this Act, grant a temporary firearms licence to the applicant for a
period not exceeding three (3) months if he considers the applicant to be a fit and proper person to
hold a firearm licence.”
