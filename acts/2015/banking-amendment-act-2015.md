ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
THE BANKING (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2015
No. 16 of 2015

AN ACT to amend the Banking Act, 2015.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Banking (Amendment) Act, 2015.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act—
“principal Act” means the Banking Act, 2015
3\. Amendment of section 153
The principal Act is amended in section 153 as follows—
(a)

by inserting after paragraph (a) of subsection (2) thereof the following new
paragraph—
“(b) severance payments;”;

No.16 of 2015

(b)

by repealing the words “severance payments” before the word “wages” in paragraph
(b);

(c)

by renumbering paragraph “(b)” as paragraph “(c)” and all other subsequent
paragraphs to be renumbered in consecutive alphabetical order as follows—
paragraph “(c)” becomes paragraph “(d)”, paragraph “(d)” becomes paragraph
“(e)”, paragraph “(e)” becomes paragraph “(f)”, paragraph “(f)” becomes
paragraph “(g)”, paragraph “(g)” becomes paragraph “(h)”, paragraph “(h)”
becomes paragraph “(i)”, paragraph “(i)” becomes paragraph “(j)” and“ paragraph
(j)” becomes paragraph “(k)”;

(d)

by repealing the letter “(e)” in paragraph “(c)” and substituting the letter “(f)”;

(e)

by repealing the letters “(c)” and “(e)” in paragraph “(d)” and substituting therefor
the letters “(d)” and “(f)” respectively.

(f)

in subsection (7), by repealing the letters “(c), (d) and (e)” and substituting therefor
the letters “(d), (e) and (f)” respectively.

Passed by the House of Representatives on the
18th July, 2015.

Passed by the Senate on the 14th August, 2015.
