No. 19 of 2015
AN ACT to amend the National Honours Act, 1998.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

### 1.

Short title

This Act may be cited as the National Honours (Amendment) Act, 2015.

### 2.

Interpretation

In this Act—
“principal Act” means the National Honours Act, 1998 No. 23 of 1998.

### 3.

Amendment of section 12

The principal Act is amended in section 12 by repealing subsections (2) and (3).
