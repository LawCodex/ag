AN ACT to amend the Immigration and Passport Act, 2014.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows—

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Immigration and Passport (Amendment) Act, 2015.

### 2.

Insertion of section 24A- Grant of Amnesty.
The Immigration and Passport Act, 2014 No. 6 of 2014 is amended by the insertion of section 24A
immediately after section 24(6) as follows—
“ 24 A. (1) A person who, immediately before the coming into force of this Act is resident in
Antigua and Barbuda as an illegal immigrant may, on application to the Minister, and on payment of any arrears owed to the Government and on payment of the prescribed fee for such
application, be granted amnesty and an extension of time to remain in Antigua and Barbuda.
