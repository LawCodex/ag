AN ACT to amend the Tourism Authority Act, 2008 No. 6 of 2008.

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Tourism Authority (Amendment) Act, 2015.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act, “principal Act” means the Tourism Authority Act, 2008 No. 6 of 2008.
