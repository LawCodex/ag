### 4.

Section 32 of the principal Act is amended by deleting the
definition of airport and substituting the following -

"airport" means any defined area of land or water intended
or designated to be used either wholly or partly for the
landing, departure,movement and servicingof aircraft, and
any buildings, installations, equipment, road ways, car
parks and open spaces on or adjacent to any such area used
in connection with the airport or its administration."
