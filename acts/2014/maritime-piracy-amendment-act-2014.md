AN ACT to amend the Maritime Piracy Act (2013) No.17 of 2013.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows—

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Maritime Piracy (Amendment) Act 2014.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“principal Act” means the Maritime Piracy Act (2013) No.17 of 2013.

—————

### 3.

Amendment of Section 3- Crime of Piracy

Printed at the Government Printing Office, Antigua and Barbuda,
by Ralph George, Government Printer
— by Authority, 2015.

The principal Act is amended in section 3 by inserting after the words “Antigua and Barbuda” the
following words “or on the high seas”.
