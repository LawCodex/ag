AN ACT to amend the Workmen’s Compensation Act Cap. 475.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Workmen’s Compensation (Amendment) Act 2014.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act“principal Act” means the the Workmen’s Compensation Act Cap. 475.
