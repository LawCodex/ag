Misuse of Drugs (Amendment) Act, 2014

by deleting subsections (6) and substituting the following—
“(6) Notwithstanding any other law, if a person commits an offence which involves the
cultivation, production, possession, supply of, or is otherwise concerned with a controlled
drug which weighs 5 kilograms or less, or is 2.5 litres or less in volume, he shall be tried
summarily”; and

(b)

by deleting subsection (7) and substituting with the following—
“(7) Notwithstanding any other law, if a person commits an offence which involves the
cultivation, production, possession, supply of, or is otherwise concerned with a controlled
drug which weighs more than 5 kilograms or is more than 2.5 litres in volume, he shall be
tried on indictment”.
