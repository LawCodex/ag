### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Friendly Societies (Amendment) Act, 2014.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“principal Act” means the Friendly Societies Act Cap.184.

5\. Amendment of Section 5- Societies to which this Act applies
The principal Act is amended in section 5 by the repeal of subsection (3) and the substitution of the
following—
“(3) Societies (in this Act called benevolent societies) for any non-profit, benevolent or
charitable purpose.
For the purpose of this subsection, a non-profit organisation is an entity or association, without
shareholders, which conducts business for the benefit of the general public.”
6\. Amendment of Section 7- Conditions of Registration
Section 7 of the principal Act is amended by inserting after the words “the Registrar”, the words—
“and a copy to the Attorney General”.
7\. Amendment of Section 9- Acknowledgment of Registry
