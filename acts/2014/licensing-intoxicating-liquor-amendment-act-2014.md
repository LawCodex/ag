AN ACT to amend the Licensing (Intoxicating Liquor) Act, Cap. 249.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Licensing (Intoxicating Liquor) (Amendment) Act, 2014.

### 2.

Amendment of section 32 – Hours of closing
Section 32 of the Licensing (Intoxicating Liquor) Act, Cap. 249 is amended as follows —
(a)

by the repeal of subsection (1) and the substitution of the following —

(1) Subject as hereinafter provided all licensed premises on which intoxicating liquor
are sold, exposed or kept for sale by retail except those premises in respect of
