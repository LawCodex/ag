ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
SOCIAL PROTECTION (AMENDMENT) ACT 2022
No. 13 of 2022
AN ACT to amend the Social Protection Act 2020, No. 22 of 2020
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows1. Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Social Protection (Amendment) Act, 2022.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act –
“principal Act” means the Social Protection Act 2020, No. 22 of 2020.
3\. Amendment of section 39 – Composition of the Commission
Section 39 of the principal Act is amended by repealing subsection (4) thereof and replacing it as
follows –
“(4) Cabinet shall appoint one of the Commissioners to be Chairperson of the Commission.”
