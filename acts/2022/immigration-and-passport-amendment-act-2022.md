ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
THE IMMIGRATION AND PASSPORT (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2022
No 9 of 2022
AN ACT to amend the Immigration and Passport Act 2014 and for other connected purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows —
1\. Short Title
This Act may be cited as the Immigration and Passport (Amendment) Act, 2022.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act,
“principal Act” means the Immigration and Passport Act, 2014 No. 6 of 2014
3\. Amendment of section 24A – Grant of Amnesty
Section 24A of the principal Act is repealed and replaced as follows –

“24A. Grant of Amnesty
(1) A person who is residing in Antigua and Barbuda as an illegal immigrant, may apply to the
Minister for grant of amnesty.
(2) The application shall:
(a)

be submitted at the Immigration Department during the amnesty period being
March 1st, 2022 to April 30th, 2022; and

(b)

be accompanied by a processing fee of $200.00.

(3) The Minister, after consultation with the Chief Immigration Officer, may grant the
application for amnesty.
(4) A person whose application for amnesty has been approved shall within 14 days of such
approval attend the Immigration Department to have his status in Antigua and Barbuda regularised
and to apply for an extension of time.
(5) For the purpose of this section –
(a)

“amnesty” is an act of pardon done under the authority of the Government that
gives an illegal immigrant, during the amnesty period, an opportunity to become a
legal immigrant lawfully residing in Antigua and Barbuda.

(b)

“regularise” is the process whereby any period during which a person resided
illegally in Antigua and Barbuda prior to the grant of amnesty is disregarded such
that the person is no longer liable to suffer any consequence under the Act for the
period that he was illegally residing in Antigua and Barbuda.”

4\. Commencement
This amendment shall be deemed to have come into operation on the 1st day of March 2022.
