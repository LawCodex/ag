ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION (OCTOBER 2015
TO DECEMBER 2015) ACT 2022
No. 15 of 2022
AN ACT to provide Supplementary Appropriation for the services of Antigua and Barbuda for the
year 2015 and for other connected purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows –
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation (October 2015 to December 2015)
Act 2022.
2\. Supplementary Appropriation of the sum of Thirty-two Million Two Hundred and
Thirty-five Thousand Five Hundred and Seventy-two ($32,235,572.)
The sums set forth in the Schedule amounting to the sum of Thirty-two Million, Two Hundred and
Thirty-five thousand, Five Hundred and Seventy-two dollars ($32,235,572.) which sums have
been drawn from the Consolidated Fund and have been paid for the services of Antigua and
Barbuda during the period October 2015 to December 2015 in addition to the sums granted in the
Appropriation Act 2015 and the Supplementary Appropriation Act 2015, No. 28 of 2015 are
ratified and deemed to have been properly paid and appropriated out of the Consolidated Fund for
the sevices mentioned in the Schedule.
