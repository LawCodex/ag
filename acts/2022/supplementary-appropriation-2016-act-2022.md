ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION (2016) ACT, 2022
No. 16 of 2022
AN ACT to provide Supplementary Appropriation for the services of Antigua and Barbuda for the
year 2016
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows—

### 1.

Short title

This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation (2016) Act 2022.

### 2.

Supplementary Appropriation of the sum of One Hundred and Eighty-seven Million
Four Hundred and Eleven Thousand Eight Hundred and Seventy. ($187,411,870.)

The sums set forth in the Schedule amounting to the sum of One Hundred and Eighty-seven Million,
Four Hundred and Eleven Thousand, Eight Hundred and Seventy dollars ($187,411,870.) which
sums have been drawn from the Consolidated Fund and have been paid for the services of Antigua
and Barbuda during the year 2016 in addition to the sums granted in the Appropriation Act 2016 are
ratified and deemed to have been properly paid and appropriated out of the Consolidated Fund for
the sevices mentioned in the Schedule.
