ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
LIMITATION (AMENDMENT) ACT 2022
No. 6 of 2022
AN ACT to amend the provisions of the Limitation Act No. 8 of 1997 to create a reasonable time
frame for legal actions in the Industrial Court to promote justice and business operation longevity.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows―

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Limitation (Amendment) Act 2022.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act –
“principal Act” means the Limitation Act 1997, No. 8 of 1997.

### 3.

Insertion of Section 7A -Time limit for trade dispute actions
The principal Act is amended by inserting immediately after section 7 the following new
section as follows —
“7A -Time limit for trade dispute actions

No action arising from a trade dispute shall be brought before the Industrial Court after the
expiration of six years from the date on which the cause of action accrued.”
