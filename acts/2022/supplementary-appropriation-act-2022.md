ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION (2019) ACT 2022
No. 19 of 2022
AN ACT to provide Supplementary Appropriation for the services of Antigua and Barbuda for the
year 2019
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows —

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation (2019) Act 2022.

### 2.

Supplementary Appropriation of the sum of Two Hundred and Twenty-three Million Five
Hundred and Thirty-three Thousand Nine Hundred and Thirteen ($223,533,913.)
Dollars
The sums set forth in the Schedule amounting to the sum of Two Hundred and Twenty-three
Million, Five Hundred and Thirty-three Thousand, Nine Hundred and Thirteen dollars
($223,533,913.) which sums have been drawn from the Consolidated Fund and have been paid for
the services of Antigua and Barbuda during the year 2019 in addition to the sums granted in the
Appropriation Act 2019 are ratified and deemed to have been properly paid and appropriated out
of the Consolidated Fund for the sevices mentioned in the Schedule.
