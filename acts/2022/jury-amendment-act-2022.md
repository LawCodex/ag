JURY (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2022
No. 10 of 2022
AN ACT to amend the Jury Act 2009, No. 6 of 2009 and for other connected purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

---

### 1.

Short title

This Act may be cited as the Jury (Amendment) Act 2022.

### 2.

Interpretation

In this Act, “principal Act” means the Jury Act 2009, No. 6 of 2009.

### 3.

Amendment of section 28– Verdict

Section 28 subsection (2) of the principal Act is amended by repealing the words “at least 10” and
replacing these with the words “at least 7”.
