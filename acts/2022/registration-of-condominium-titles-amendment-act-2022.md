Registration of Condominium Titles (Amendment) Act, 2022.

REGISTRATION OF CONDOMINIUM TITLES (AMENDMENT) ACT 2022

AN ACT to amend the Registration of Condominium Titles Act, Cap. 376 and for other connected
purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows1.

Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Registration of Condominium Titles (Amendment) Act, 2022.
2.

Interpretation

In this Act –
“principal Act” means the Registration of Condominium Titles Act, Cap. 376.
3.

Amendment of section 7 – Requirements of a condominium plan

Section 7 of the principal Act is amended by—
(a) repealing subsection (2) in its entirety and replacing this with the following –
“(2) A condominium plan located in the island of Antigua which is lodged for registration
shall be accompanied by such certificate and other documents as may be prescribed.”
(b) inserting after subsection (2), the following new subsections (3) and (4) and renumbering
(3), (4) and (5) as (5), (6) and (7) –
“(3) A condominium plan located on the island of Barbuda which is lodged for
registration shall be accompanied by:
(a) a non-citizen land-holding licence issued by the Governor General, if required;
(b) the survey plan authenticated by the Chief Surveyor; and
(c) any registrable interest.”
(4) The Registrar shall, on receipt of the condominium plan filed under subsection 6 –

(a) create special registers required in accordance with the condominium plan; and
(b) issue the certificates thereunder.”
4.

Amendment of section 16 – Regulations

Section 16 of the principal Act is amended by repealing the words, “Governor General” and
replacing these with the word “Minister”.
