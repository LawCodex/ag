PESTICIDES AND TOXIC CHEMICALS (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2022
No. 11 of 2022
AN ACT to amend the Pesticides and Toxic Chemicals Act, No 12 of 2008.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Pesticides and Toxic Chemicals (Amendment) Act, 2022.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“principal Act” means the Pesticides and Toxic Chemicals Act, No 12 of 2008.

### 3.

Amendment of section 3-Establishment and Constitution of the Board.
(1) Section 3 of the principal Act is amended by repealing subsection (1) and substituting the
following—
“(1) There is hereby established a Board to be known as the Pesticides and Toxic Chemicals
Control Board which shall be a body corporate with perpetual succession and a common seal
and shall be capable of—
(a)

acquiring, holding and disposing of real and personal property;

(b)

suing and being sued; and

(c)

doing and suffering such acts and things as bodies corporate may lawfully do and
suffer.”
