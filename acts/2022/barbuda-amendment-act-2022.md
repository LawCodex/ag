BARBUDA (AMENDMENT) ACT 2022
No. 23 of 2022

AN ACT to amend the Barbuda Act, Cap. 42 and for other connected purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows―

---

### 1.

Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Barbuda (Amendment) Act, 2022.

### 2.

Interpretation

In this Act –
“principal Act” means the Barbuda Act, Cap. 42.

### 3.

Amendment of section 58 – Rent payable

Section 58 of the principal Act is repealed and replaced as follows –

No.

23 of 2022

“58. Rent payable
The rents payable under any lease of land granted by the crown in the Island of Barbuda shall be
paid to the Accountant General for the benefit of the Council unless the Cabinet, in consultation
with the Council, directs otherwise.”
