SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION (2018) ACT, 2022
No. 18 of 2022

AN ACT to provide Supplementary Appropriation for the services of Antigua and Barbuda for the
year 2018
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows —

### 1.

Short title

This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation (2018) Act 2022.

### 2.

Supplementary Appropriation of the sum of One Hundred and Ninety-eight Million One
Hundred and Sixty-two Thousand Two Hundred and Twenty-two ($198,162,222.) Dollars

Supplementary Appropriation Act, 2022.

The sums set forth in the Schedule amounting to the sum of One Hundred and Ninety-eight Million,
One Hundred and Sixty-two Thousand, Two Hundred and Twenty-two dollars ($198,162,222.)
which sums have been drawn from the Consolidated Fund and have been paid for the services of
Antigua and Barbuda during the year 2018 in addition to the sums granted in the Appropriation Act
2018 are ratified and deemed to have been properly paid and appropriated out of the Consolidated
Fund for the sevices mentioned in the Schedule.
