AN ACT to amend the Vehicles and Road Traffic Act Cap 460.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Vehicles and Road Traffic (Amendment) Act, 2011.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“notice” has the same meaning assigned to it by Section 2 of the Vehicles and Road Traffic
(Enforcement and Administration) Act Cap 461;
“principal Act” means the Vehicles and Road Traffic Act, Cap 460.

4\. Insertion of sub-section 66 (8) and (9)
The principal Act is amended in section 66 by inserting the following after sub-section (7)—
“(8) Where a police officer, upon inspecting a motor vehicle, determines that a window or windscreen
of the motor vehicle has applied to it any tint, film, commercial sign or decal that is not in compliance
with Regulation 21A or 21B of the Vehicles and Road Traffic Regulations, he shall—
(a)

inform the driver of the motor vehicle that the window or windscreen of the motor vehicle
has tint, film, commercial sign or decal applied that is not in compliance with Regulation
21A or 21B of the Vehicles and Road Traffic Regulations;

(b)

serve on the driver of the motor vehicle, in the manner prescribed in the Vehicles and
Road Traffic (Enforcement and Administration) Act, a notice that the vehicle has tint,
film, commercial sign or decal applied to the window or windscreen that is not in
compliance with Regulation 21A or 21B of the Vehicles and Road Traffic Regulations;

(c)

in the absence of the driver, affix in the manner prescribed in the Vehicles and Road
Traffic (Enforcement and Administration) Act, a notice that the vehicle has tint, film
commercial sign or decal applied to the window or windscreen that is not in compliance
with Regulation 21A or 21B of the Vehicles and Road Traffic Regulations.

(9) The Traffic Commissioner shall send to the General Manager of the Transport Board, a duplicate
of the notice which shall contain the period of time, by which the owner of a vehicle shall be required
to remedy the incorrect tint, film, commercial sign or decal and present his vehicle for inspection.
