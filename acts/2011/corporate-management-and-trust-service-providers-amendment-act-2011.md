AN ACT to amend the Corporate Management and Trust Service Providers Act, 2008 No. 20 of 2008
and for incidental and connected purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Corporate Management and Trust Service Providers (Amendment) Act,
2011.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act “the principal Act” means the Corporate Management and Trust Service Providers Act,
2008 No. 20 of 2008.

### 3.

Amendment of section 5
The principal Act is amended in section 5(3) by repealing the words “Schedule J” and substituting the
words “the Regulations”.
