No. 8 of 2011
AN ACT to amend the Antigua and Barbuda Labour Code, Cap. 27 and for incidental and connected
purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Antigua and Barbuda Labour Code (Amendment) Act, 2010 and shall
come into operation on a date that the Minister may, by Notice published in the Gazette, appoint.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act “the principal Act” means the Antigua and Barbuda Labour Code, Cap. 27.

### 3.

Amendment of section F3

________
Printed at the Government Printing Office, Antigua and Barbuda,
by Paget Terry, Acting Government Printer
— By Authority, 2011.

The principal Act is amended in section F3 by repealing paragraph (g) and substituting the following—
“(g) a national of a Member State who has been granted entry into Antigua and Barbuda in
accordance with section 4 or section 5 of the Caribbean Community Skilled Nationals Act,
1997.”
