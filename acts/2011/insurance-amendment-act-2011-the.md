Amendment of section 202 - Periodic examination of companies and affiliates

The principal Act is amended in section 202 by inserting after subsection (7), the following—
“(8) An insurer shall be subject to onsite examinations by the Commission at its local office,
affiliate or any other office deemed appropriate at least once a year to ensure that the company
is in compliance with this Act, the Money Laundering (Prevention) Act and the Prevention of
Terrorism Act.”
