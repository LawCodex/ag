AN ACT to amend the International Business Corporations Act, Cap 222.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

### 1.

Short title.
This Act may be cited as the International Business Corporations (Amendment) Act, 2011.

### 2.

Amendment of Section 316.
Section 316 of the International Business Corporations Act is amended by the repeal of subsection
(5) and the substitution of the following –
“(5) The Commission shall be managed by a Board of Directors consisting of seven (7) persons,
all of whom shall be appointed by the Minister after approval of the Cabinet”.
