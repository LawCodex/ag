No. 5 of 2003
AN ACT to amend the Representation of the People Act.

[ 24th April, 2003

]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as
follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Representation of the People short title.
(Amendment) (No. 2) Act, 2003.

### 2.

Section 18 of the Representation of the People Act is Amendment of
amended in paragraph (a) of subsection (1A) by the deletion of section 18 Cap.
the words "the Commission shall publish the register not later 379.
than 31st March, 2003" and the substitution of the words "the
Commission shall publish the register not later than 30th September, 2003."
