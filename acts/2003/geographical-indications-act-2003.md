### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Geographical Indications Act,
2002 and shall come into force on a clay appointed by the Minister
by notice published in the Gazette.

### 2.

In this Act,
"Court" means the High Court;
"geographical indication" means an indication which identifies a good as originating in the territory of a country, or
a region or locality in that territory, where a given quality,
reputation or other characteristic of the good is essentially
attributable to its geographical origin;
