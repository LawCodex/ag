ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA

No.26 of 2003

AN ACT to anlend the Money Laundering Prevention Act, 1996.

[5th February, 20041

ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as
follows-

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Money Laundering Prevention (Amendment) Act, 2003.

Short title.

### 2.

The Money Laundering Prevention Act 1996 is amended
by the repeal of subsection 12(1) and the substitution of the
following-

Amendment of
12.

"(1) A financial institution shall, subject to
section 12A, retain, or retain a copy of, each
customer generated financial transaction
document for the minimum retention period
applicable to the document."
