ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
THE CONSTITUTION OF ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2025
NO. 22 of 2025

AN ACT to amend the Constitution of Antigua and Barbuda, as contained in Schedule 1 to the
Antigua and Barbuda Constitution Order 1981, Cap 23
WHEREAS Section 47(1) of the Constitution of Antigua and Barbuda (“the Constitution’) provides
that Parliament may alter any of the provisions of this Constitution or of the Supreme Court order
in the manner specified in this section
AND WHEREAS Section 47(2) of the Constitution provides that a bill to alter this Constitution or
the Supreme Court Order shall not be regarded as being passed by the House unless on its final
reading in the House the Bill is supported by the votes of not less than two-thirds of all members of
the House;
AND WHEREAS this is a Bill to amend the Constitution
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

Short title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the Constitution of Antigua and Barbuda (Amendment) Act 2025.

Constitution of Antiguaand Barbuda (Amendment) Act, 2025

(2) This Act shall come into effect on a day to be appointed by the Minister by Order published
in the Gazette.

Repeal and replacement of Schedule 3

Schedule 3 of the Constitution of Antigua and Barbuda is repealed and replaced as follows –

## SCHEDULE 3 TO THE CONSTITUTION

Section 127 (1)
OATHS AND AFFIRMATIONS

Oath (or Affirmation) of Allegiance
I, ……………………………. [swear by Almighty God]/[solemnly and sincerely, declare and
affirm] that I will faithfully bear true allegiance to the State of Antigua and Barbuda, its Constitution
and laws.
So help me God (To be omitted in affirmation).

Oath (or Affirmation) of Office
1, …………………….. [swear by Almighty God]/[solemnly and sincerely declare and affirm] that
I will honour, uphold and preserve the Constitution of Antigua and Barbuda and the law of my
nation, that I will conscientiously, impartially and to the best of my ability discharge my duties as
…………………….. and do right to all manner of people without fear or favour, affection or illwill.
So help me God (To be omitted in affirmation).

Oath (or Affirmation) of Secrecy
1, …………………….. [swear by Almighty God]/[solemnly and sincerely declare and affirm] that
I will not on any account, at any time whatsoever, disclose any counsel, advice, opinion or vote
given by any Minister as [a member of the Cabinet]/[name of post/office] and that I will not, except
with the authority of [the Minister]/[the Cabinet] and to such extent as may be required for the proper
conduct of the Government of Antigua and Barbuda, directly or indirectly reveal the business or

proceedings of [the Cabinet]/[my office] or any matter to my knowledge as [a member of
Cabinet]/[post/office].
So help me God (To be omitted in affirmation).
