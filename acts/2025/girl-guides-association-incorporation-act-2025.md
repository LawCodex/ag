ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
GIRL GUIDES ASSOCIATION OF ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
(INCORPORATION) ACT 2025
No. 9 of 2025

AN ACT An Act to incorporate the Girl Guides Association of Antigua and Barbuda.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows―

1.

Short title

(1) This Act may be cited as the Girl Guides Association of Antigua And Barbuda
(Incorporation) Act 2025.
(2) This Act shall come into operation on a date appointed by the Minister by Notice published
in the Gazette.
2.

Interpretation

In this Act—
“Association” means the Girl Guides Association of Antigua and Barbuda incorporated under
this Act;

“Council” means the Council constituted under section 5;
“former Association” means the unincorporated association known as “The Girl Guides
Association of Antigua and Barbuda” existing immediately before the commencement of this
Act;
“Girl Guide” means a girl guide recognized as such under this Act or the constitution, byelaws or rules of the Association and includes all officers of the Association.
3.

Association to be a body corporate

The Association is hereby established as a body corporate with perpetual succession and a
common seal.

4.

Objects

The objects of the Association are to—
(a) promote, foster and maintain high standards of girl guiding in Antigua and Barbuda;
(b) safeguard and enhance the standing, interests and reputation of girl guides;
(c) encourage and assist in providing facilities for the practice of girl guiding;
(d) assist members and past members of the Association who are in need;
(e) affiliate with any regional or extra-regional body having objects similar to those of
the Association;
(f) do anything necessary or incidental to the carrying out of the objects specified in
paragraphs (a) to (e).
5.

Management of the Association

(1) There shall be a Council which shall be responsible for the management of the
Association.
(2) The Council shall initially consist of the persons who, at the commencement of this Act,
are members of the Committee of the former Association, and thereafter, the composition of the
Council shall be determined by election or appointment in accordance with rules made under
section 7.
(3) The Council may establish or otherwise provide for divisions, detachments, companies,
units, or branches of girl guides throughout Antigua and Barbuda as it considers necessary or
expedient to achieve the Association’s objects.
(4) The Council may delegate any of its functions to one or more committees, with or without
authority for those committees to further delegate any of those functions.

(5) No member of the Council, or of a committee or sub-committee, shall be personally liable
for any act or default of the Council, committee, or sub-committee, done or omitted to be done in
good faith in the exercise or purported exercise of the powers conferred by this Act.
6.

Validity of meetings, etc., of Council

No act, decision, or proceeding of the Council under this Act is invalid solely because—
(a) there is a vacancy in the Council’s membership or a defect in its composition; or
(b) a procedural omission, default, or irregularity has occurred, provided that such
omission, default, or irregularity does not affect the substance of the decision or
proceeding.
7.

Rules
(1) The Council may make rules respecting—
(a) the composition of, and manner of election or appointment of members of the
association to, the council;
(b) the prescribing of qualifications for the admission of members to the association;
(c) the organization of the functions of divisions, detachments, companies, units or
branches of the association;
(d) the appointment and duties of the officers and employees of the association;
(e) the custody of the property of the association and the keeping and auditing of
accounts;
(f) the collection and disposal of the funds of the association;
(g) the discipline of members of the association;
(h) the organization of courses of training for girl guides;
(i) the use of the facilities of the association;
(j) the uniform, token, emblem, badge, or other distinguishing mark to be adopted by
the association; and
(k) any other matter it considers necessary for the carrying out of the purposes and the
good management of the association.
(2) Where any rule conflicts with this Act, this Act shall prevail.

8.

Appropriation of assets

(1) The income and property of the Association, however derived, shall be used solely for the
purposes of promoting the objects of the Association as specified in section 5, and no portion of
that income or property shall be paid or transferred directly or indirectly by way of dividend,
bonus or in any other manner to members of the Association.
(2) Notwithstanding subsection (1), nothing prevents –
(a) The payment in good faith of remuneration to a member, servant or an officer of the
Association for service rendered to the Association; or
(b) A payment of a benevolent nature in pursuance of the objects of the Association
specified in section 4(d).

9.

Address of Association
(1) The Association shall maintain a permanent address in Antigua and Barbuda for the
service of documents on the Association.
(2) Where address referred to in subsection (1) changes, the Association shall notify the
Registrar, or other relevant authority, in writing within 30 days, and may publish notice
of the change in the Gazette.

10\. Sealing of Documents
Any document issued by the Association which is required by law to be under seal shall be sealed
with the common seal of the Association and witnessed by the Secretary and two other members
of the Council.
11\. Vesting of assets, liabilities, etc., of former Association
(1) Upon the commencement of this Act, all the assets and liabilities of the former
Association shall vest in the Association.
(2) All deeds, bonds, instruments, and contracts in force immediately before the
commencement of this Act, to which the former Association was a party, remain in full force and
effect and are enforceable against or in favour of the Association as though the Association had
originally been a party to them.
(3) All assets held by the former Association for the benefit of any of its divisions shall vest in
the Association and continue to be held for the corresponding division’s purposes.

12\. Offences
(1) A person commits an offence if that person—
(a) forms, organizes, or participates in any group or entity that unlawfully claims,
purports, or represents itself as girl guides or as being affiliated with the Association;
(b) assists in the formation or operation of any group or entity that unlawfully claims,
purports, or represents itself as girl guides or as being affiliated with the Association;
(c) falsely represents or allows themselves to be represented as a member, officer, or
agent of the Association for the purpose of soliciting, collecting, or receiving money or
other benefits; or
(d) without the Association’s authorization, wears, displays, or uses any uniform, token,
emblem, badge, or distinguishing mark, or any imitation thereof, adopted by the
Association, in a manner that suggests membership in or affiliation with the Association.

(2) A person convicted of an offence under this section is liable on summary conviction to a
fine not exceeding ($2000) two thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding
six months, or to both.
