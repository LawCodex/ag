VEHICLES AND ROAD TRAFFIC (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2025
NO. 1 of 2025
AN ACT to amend the Vehicles and Road Traffic Act, Cap. 460 and for other connected
purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

---

### 1.

Short title and commencement
This Act may be cited as the Vehicles and Road Traffic (Amendment) Act, 2025.

[This Act shall come into operation on a date to be appointed by the Minister by Notice
published in the Gazette.]

Interpretation

In this Act –
“principal Act” means the Vehicles and Road Traffic Act, Cap. 460.

Amendment of Section 2 – Interpretation

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by –
(a) repealing the definition of “Commissioner” in its entirety;
(b) inserting in its appropriate alphabetical order the following definition –
“General Manager of Transport Board” means the person appointed as the General Manager
under section 9 of the Transport Board Act, 1995;
“SUV” means sports utility vehicle.
“Transport Board” means the Transport Board established under the section 3 of the Transport
Board Act 1995, No. 13 of 1995.

### 4.

Amendment of the principal Act

The principal Act is amended in sections 15-20, 22, 23, 26-29, 36,37,39, 51,52, 72, 73, 79, 87 and
91 by deleting the words “Commissioner of Inland Revenue” wherever occurring and substituting
therefor the words “General Manager of Transport Board”.

### 5.

Amendment of section 5 – Duties of General Manager of Transport Board

Section 5 of the principal Act is amended by repealing the section in its entirety and replacing it
with the following –
“5. Duties of General Manager of Transport Board
It shall be the duty of the General Manager of Transport Board in accordance with the provisions
of this Act(a) to issue licences in respect of vehicles;
(b) to keep a register of licensed motor vehicles;
(c) to keep a register of animal drawn vehicles and pedal bicycles; and
(d) to perform all other duties that may be conferred or imposed on him by this or any other
Act or by the regulations made thereunder.”

### 6.

Amendment of section 6 – Duties of Examining Officer

Section 6 of the principal Act is amended by repealing the section and replacing it as follows –
“6. Duties of Examining Officer
It shall be the duty of an Examining Officer, when required by the Traffic Commissioner or the
General Manager of Transport Board to –

(a) examine and report upon the condition, construction and equipment of any motor
vehicle or other vehicles;
(b) test applicants for drivers' licences as to their competence to drive, and, if satisfied, to
grant the necessary certificates of competency; and
(c) perform all such other duties as are or may be conferred or imposed upon him by this
or any other Act or by any regulations made thereunder.”

### 7.

Amendment of section 7 – Fees and Duties

Section 7 of the principal Act is amended by repealing the section and replacing it with the
following –
“7. Fees and Duties
(1) The fees and duties specified in the First Schedule shall be payable to the Transport
Board in respect of the several matters to which they are applicable.
(2) All fees and duties received by the Transport Board shall be used –
(a) for the payment of capital expenses and operating costs;
(b) payment of loans for infrastructural development; and
(c) any surplus remaining after the above shall be paid into the Infrastructure
Development Fund.
(3) The Cabinet may by Order, subject to an affirmative resolution of the House, amend,
vary, suspend or revoke any or all of the provisions of the First Schedule.
(4) The collection of fees by the Transport Board from the 1 st day of January 2011 to the date
of commencement of this Act is deemed to be in accordance with the provisions of this
Act.

### 8.

Amendment of section 73 – Removal of permanent disqualification

Section 73 of the principal act is amended in subsection (6) by repealing the subsection and
substituting the following new subsection –
“(6) Where an application is made under this section the applicant shall pay to the
Transport Board such sum as may be prescribed in the Schedule.”
