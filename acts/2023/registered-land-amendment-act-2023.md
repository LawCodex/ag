ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
REGISTERED LAND (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2023
No. 8 of 2023
AN ACT to amend the Registered Land Act, Cap. 374 to make provision for compulsory
registration by a person entitled to be registered as a proprietor of any parcel of Land registered
in the name of the Crown after the cadastral survey in 1976, to revise the procedures relating to
the mutation of the land register and the subdivision of contiguous parcels.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Registered Land (Amendment) Act, 2023

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“principal Act” means the Registered Land Act, Cap. 374

### 3.

Amendment of section 3-Reconciliation with other laws
Section 3 of the principal Act is amended by repealing subsection (2) in its entirety and replacing
this with the following –
“(2) The provisions of this Act shall apply only to land, interests in land or dealings in land
within the State of Antigua and Barbuda that are registered under this Act.”

### 4.

Amendment of section 21-Combinations and subdivisions
Section 21 of the principal Act is amended by:
(a)

Repealing the proviso in its entirety and replacing it with the following –
“(i) nothing shall be done under this section which would be inconsistent with
this Act or any other law; and
(ii)

(b)

where a proprietor is subdividing his parcel for the purpose of building
development, the Registrar may require him to submit a plan of the
proposed subdivisions prepared by a surveyor licensed under the Land
Surveyors Act and certified by the appropriate authority as conforming
with the requirements of any planning law for the time being in force.”

repealing subsection (3) in its entirety and replacing it with the following –
“(3) Upon the application of the proprietor of a parcel for division of his parcel
into two or more parcels, the Registrar may by order approve the division and
shall cause notice of the order to be forwarded to the Chief Land Surveyor on a
mutation form for an amendment to the Survey Map, provided that, the Registrar
shall not approve an application for a subdivision of a parcel which is subject to a
lease, unless the application is accompanied by the written consent of the lessor
or, in the case of a parcel subject to a sub-lease, by the written consent of the head
lessor.”

### 5.

Amendment of Section 121A – Registration of proprietor entitled to Crown land
Section 121A of the principal Act is amended as follows—
(a) by repealing subsection (1) thereof and substituting the following:
“(1) Where a person –
is entitled to apply to be registered as proprietor of a parcel of land that has been
registered in the name of the Crown, that person shall apply to the Registrar to be
registered as proprietor in respect of that parcel of land within three (3) years
from the coming into force of the provisions of this Act; and
(b)

has submitted an application pursuant to paragraph (a), the Registrar shall allow
that person such reasonable time as is necessary to provide evidence on affidavit
and such other documents as the Registrar may require.”

(b) by inserting after subsection (7) the following new subsections:
“(8) No person, except the Crown, shall, after the expiration of the time specified in
subsection (1), have any dealings with land that continues to be registered in the name of the
Crown.

(9) Where, any parcel of land is registered in the name of the Crown after the Cadastral Survey
and marked “no dealing”, and remains so registered after the expiration of the time specified in
subsection (1), the parcel of land shall be vested in the Crown.
(10) Upon the expiration of the date specified in subsection (1), the Chief Land Officer shall
review the status of the parcels affected by the notation of no dealing and make a report, of his
findings, including any recommendations for the consideration of the Cabinet.
(11) Where upon receipt of the Chief Land Officer’s report, the Cabinet considers it necessary to
dispose of such parcels, the Cabinet may, by order published in the Official Gazette, authorize
the Chief Land Officer to deal with the parcels in the manner directed by the Cabinet.”
