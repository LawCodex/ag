AN ACT to amend the National Honours Act.
[ 27th December. 2001 ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as
follows -

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the National Honours (Amendment) Act 2001.

short title.

### 2.

The Schedule to the National Honours Act 1998, No. 23 Amendment of
'Schedule.
of 1998 is amended as follows -

(a)

in paragraph 2 by the repeal of subparagraph (1)
and the substitution of the following "(1) The Most Distinguished Order of the Nation may be awarded to any citizen of Antigua and
Barbuda or to a citizen of any other country who
has rendered htinguished and outstanding service
to Antigua and Barbuda, the Caricom region or to
the international community."
