(1) It shall be a defence to any action brought against any

under this Act that the compliance was in accordance with the
provisions of this Act.

respect to anything done or omitted to be done in good faith in
connection with the administration of, or pursuant to, and in
reliance on this Act or any regulation, instruction, or direction
issued under this Act.
