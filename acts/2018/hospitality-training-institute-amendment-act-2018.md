ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
HOSPITALITY TRAINING INSTITUTE (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2018
No. 16 of 2018

AN ACT to amend the Hospitality Training Institute Act, 2006, No. 18 of 2006.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

---

### 1.

Short title

This Act may be cited as the Hospitality Training Institute (Amendment) Act, 2018.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act, “principal Act” means the Hospitality Training Institute Act, 2006, No. 18 of 2006.
Amendment of section 2
Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of the definition of “Minister” and the
substitution of the following—
“Minister” means the Minister with responsibility for Education.
