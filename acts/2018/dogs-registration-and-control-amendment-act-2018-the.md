ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as Follow:

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as The Dog Registration and Control (Amendment) Act, 2018.

### 2.

Amendment of section 2
The Dogs Registration and Control Act 2006, No 12 of 2006, hereafter referred to as the principal
Act is amended in section 2, by deleting the definition of “Minister” and substituting the
following“Minister” means the Minister with responsibility for Health;”.
