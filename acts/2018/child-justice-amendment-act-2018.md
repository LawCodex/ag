ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
CHILD JUSTICE (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2018
No. 4 of 2018
AN ACT to amend the Child Justice Act 2015, No. 23 of 2015 to raise the age of criminal
responsibility from 8 years to 12 years in keeping with regional standards and for other related and
connected purposes.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Child Justice (Amendment) Act, 2018.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act ―
“principal Act” means the Child Justice Act 2015, No. 23 of 2015.
The principal Act is amended in section 5 by repealing the words “of eight years” wherever it
occurs and replacing these with the words “of twelve years”.

.

Section 43 of the principal Act is amended in paragraph (a) by repealing the words “between 8
years and 12 years” and replacing these with the words “under fourteen years”.

### 5.

Amendment of section 52 of the principal Act
Section 52 of the principal Act is amended ―
(a)

in subsection (1) by repealing the words “of eight years” and replacing these with
the words “of twelve years”; and

(b)

in subsection (3) by repealing the words “of subsection (3)” and replacing these
with the words “of subsection (2)”.
