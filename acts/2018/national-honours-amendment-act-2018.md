ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
NATIONAL HONOURS (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2018
No. 5 of 2018
AN ACT to amend the National Honours Act, 1998 No. 23 of 1998 and to make adequate
financial provision for the proper maintenance of the status of the recipient of the order of
National Hero.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as National Honours (Amendment) Act, 2018.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act, a reference to the “principal Act” means the National Honours Act 1998 No. 23 of
1998.
3\. Insertion of a new section 15A – Benefits and Entitlements
The principal Act is amended by inserting immediately after section 15 a new section 15A to be
worded as follows:

“15A. Benefits and Entitlements
(1) A person who is appointed to the Most Exalted Order of National Hero is entitled to receive
during his or her lifetime the benefits and entitlements provided in this section ―
(a)

an annual sinecure of ninety-six thousand Eastern Caribbean
(EC$96,000.00) payable in equal monthly instalments in arrears;

dollars

(b)

an exemption from the payment of taxes, levies and contributions payable under
any law in force in Antigua and Barbuda;

(c)

exemption from the payment of customs duties payable on goods imported only
for his or her personal use at his or her personal residence up to a maximum of
twelve thousand dollars Eastern Caribbean Currency (EC$12,000.00) per year;

(d)

exemption from the payment of property tax on his or her residential property
where he or she normally resides;

(2) The sinecure and other benefits provided for a National Hero under this Act are not subject
to the payments of taxes, contributions and levies prescribed by any law in force in Antigua and
Barbuda.
(3) In addition to the benefits provided in subsection (1), a National Hero is also entitled to be
provided with the following―
(a)

a suitable vehicle befitting the status of a National Hero once every seven years;

(b)

one household assistant of his or her choice; and

(c)

a driver as and when needed

(4) The sinecure and benefits provided for a National Hero under this section shall be paid for
from funds appropriated by Parliament.
(5) On all national and official occasions to which a person appointed to the Order of National
Hero is invited, his or her rank shall be next in line after the Ministers of State.”
