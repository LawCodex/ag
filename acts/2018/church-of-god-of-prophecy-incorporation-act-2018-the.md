ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
THE CHURCH OF GOD OF PROPHECY (INCORPORATION) ACT 2018
No. 11 of 2018

AN ACT to provide for the Incorporation of the Church of God of Prophecy, to vest certain lands
therein and to provide for matters incidental thereto.
WHEREAS the Church of God of Prophecy is an international fellowship of Christian Believers
with international headquarters at Bible Place, Cleveland, Tennessee, United States of America;
WHEREAS the Church of God of Prophecy has been serving the people of Antigua and Barbuda
since 1950; and
WHEREAS the Church acquired lands and built churches and currently there are eight other
churches affiliated to the Church of God of Prophecy throughout Antigua and Barbuda; and
WHEREAS the Church has continued its programme of expanding its ministry and providing
educational programmes and social activities training through its local headquarters located in
Rowan Henry Street, Gambles; and

No.11 of 2018

WHEREAS lands and hereditaments have been at different times conveyed in trust for the
purposes of said Church and its constituents, or are now beneficially held, used, occupied or
enjoyed for such purposes by the same, or the officials or members thereof and
WHEREAS it is desirable and expedient that the Church of God of Prophecy shall have the right
to acquire, hold, deal with and dispose of movable and immovable property in Antigua and
Barbuda for the purposes of the Church of God of Prophecy.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Church of God of Prophecy (Incorporation) Act, 2018
2\. Incorporation
The Church of God of Prophecy is hereby constituted a body corporate under the name and title of
“The Church of God Of Prophecy” with perpetual succession and a common seal and with power
and authority by that name to sue and be sued, to acquire and to hold real and personal property
for all purposes connected with the Church of God of Prophecy in Antigua and Barbuda and with
power to sell, mortgage, alienate, lease, exchange or otherwise dispose of the said property.
3\. Execution of leases, transfers, contracts by Church
All deeds of conveyance or re-conveyance, documents by mortgages, charges, assignments or
other deeds and documents whatsoever and all powers of attorney and all notarial instruments
required to be executed by the said Church of God of Prophecy shall be deemed sufficiently
executed if sealed with its common seal and subscribed by the District Overseer of the Church of
God of Prophecy for the time being and such execution shall be valid and effectual at law.
4\. Vesting
All real and personal property in Antigua and Barbuda now held by or vested in any person on
behalf of the Church of God of Prophecy in Antigua and Barbuda are hereby transferred to and
vested in the Church of God of Prophecy and its successors for the same estates and interest and to
the extent to which the same were respectively at the time of passing of this Act held or vested as
aforesaid, subject to the rights, reservations and charges if any, now affecting the same and the
trusts upon which the sane are now held, without conveyance, transfer or assignment.
Saving
Nothing in this Act contained shall affect or be deemed to affect the rights of Her Majesty, her
heirs and successors or any body, politic or corporate or any other person except such as are
mentioned in this Act and those claiming by, from or under them.
