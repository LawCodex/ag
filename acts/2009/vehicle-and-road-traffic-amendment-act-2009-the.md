ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
THE VEHICLE AND ROAD TRAFFIC (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2009
No. 7 of 2009
AN ACT to amend the Vehicle and Road Traffic Act.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as follows:
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Vehicle and Road Traffic (Amendment) Act, 2009.
2\. Amendment of section 87-Regulations
Section 87 of the Vehicle and Road Traffic Act, Cap. 460, in this Act referred to as the principal Act,
is amended as follows
(1) by inserting immediately after paragraph (i) of the following
“(j) the control, restriction or prohibition of vehicular parking on specified roads;
(k) the immobilisation and removal of vehicles which are parked without authority in places not
permitted for the parking of vehicles;”
(2) by the re-numbering of paragraph “(j)” as paragraph “(l)”.
