The principal Act is amended by inserting after section 13 the following section—
“13A

Penalties

A person who contravenes a provision of this Act commits an offence, and where no penalty is
expressly provided, is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding ten thousand dollars or
imprisonment for a term not exceeding one year.”.
