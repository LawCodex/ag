### 2.

The long title of the Transport Board Act, 1995 (in h s Act
referred to as the "principal Act") is repealed and the following long
title is substituted:
"AN ACT to establish a Transport Board to promote efficient
management and administration in ground transportation of
persons and goods in Antigua and Barbuda, to regulate and
institute proper management procedures for public service
vehicles, to advise Government on the management and
maintenance of its vehcles in an efficient and economical
manner and for connected purposes.".
