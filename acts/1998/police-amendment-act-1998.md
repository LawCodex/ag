ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as
follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Police (Amendment) Act, 1998.

### 2.

Section 2 of the Police Act (in this Act referred to as the
"principal Act") is amended by inserting the following definitions
in their alphabetical order:

July, 1993 and to which Antigua and Barbuda is a party;".

### 4.

After section 4 of the principal Act, the following new section
is inserted:
"GovemorGeneral whorise

### 4A.

(1) The Governor-General may by order
published in the Gazette direct any member of
the Force to proceed to any Caricom country for
the performance of similar duties performed by
him in his office and be subject to the orders of
the Officer in charge of the Police Force of that
other Caricom country.
(2) Any member of the Police Force of any
other Caricom country who is sent to Antigua
and Barbuda for the performance of police duties similar to duties performed by him in office
shall, while on duty in Antigua and Barbuda be
liable to perform the same duues and be subject
to the orders of the Commissionerof Police in the
same manner and in all respects as if he were a
member of the Force, and shall have the same
rights, powers, privileges and immunities as are
conferred by law and custom on members of the
Force as may be necessary for the performance
of such duties.
(3) Where a member of the Police Force of
another Caricom country who is performing
duties in Antigua and Barbuda pursuant to a
direction given under subsection (1) becomes or
is injured while in Antigua and Barbuda that
member shall be entitled to the same medical
care as if he were a member of the Force.

(4) A person who, immediately before the
coming into force of this section is a member of
the Force may not be required to proceed to any
other Caricom country without his consent in
writing having first been given.
