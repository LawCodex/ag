AN ACT to amend the Prison Act Cap. 341.
[ 1st October, 1998

1

ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as
follows1. This Act may be cited as the Prison (Amendment) Act, 1998.

### 2.

Section 2 of the Prison Act, in this Act referred to as the
principal Act, I amended by inserting the following in their
alphabetical order:
"Contracted out prison" means a prison in respect of which
there is in force for the time being a contract for the management of the prison;
"the Contractor" means the person who has been contracted
to manage the prison;
"Minister" means the Minister with responsibility for the
prison;
"prison custody officer" means a person appointed under
section 7A,".

### 3.

After section 3 of the principal Act the following section is
inserted:
"3A(1)The Governor-General on the advice
of the Minister may enter into a contract with
another person for the management by that
person of the prison.
(2) Where the Governor-General has entered
into d contract as provided under subsection (1)
the prison shall be managed subject to and in
accordance with the provisions of thePrison Act
and its Rules."

Insertion of
section 7A.

### 4.

After section 7 of the principal Act the following section is
inserted:
"7A. Where the Governor-General has entered into a contract as provided under section
3A(1), the Governor-General on the advice of
the Minister shall
(a)

appoint a superintendent to the contracted out prison to perform the
functions of the superintendent under the Prison Act and its Rule;

(b)

appoint prison custody officers to
the contracted out prison to carry
out the duties and responsibilities
of prison officers under the provisionof thePrison Act and itsRules.".
