### 5.

The Cabinet may make regulations in respect of
villages for(a) the purchase or rental of cottages and lots of

land;
( b ) the duties of Wardens;
( c ) the erection of, addition to, alteration of or
repairs to houses, cottages, buildings, fences, walls,
hedges, or other structures whatsoever;

(d) the control and good government of such villages generally;

and may by and in such regulations provide for any penalty
or penalties for any breach thereof.
