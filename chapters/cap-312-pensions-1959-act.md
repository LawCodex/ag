### 5.

(1) T h e power to grant any award under any
pensions law enacted by the Legislature and, in accordance
with any provisions in that behalf contained in any such law,
to withhold, reduce in amount or suspend any award payable
under any such law is hereby vested in the Governor-General
acting in his discretion.

(2) In this section "pensions law" means any law
