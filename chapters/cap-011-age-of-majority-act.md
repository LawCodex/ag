### 2.

(1) Subject to this Act, as from the date on which
this Act comes into operation, a person attains full age on
attaining the age of eighteen instead of on attaining th'k age

has then already attained the age of eighteen but not the
age of twenty-one.
(2) Subsection (1) applies for the purposes of any rule
of law, and in the absence of a definition or of any indication of a contrary intention, for the construction of "full age",
"infant", "infancy", "minor", "minority", and similar
expressions(a) in this Act and any other statutory provisions
