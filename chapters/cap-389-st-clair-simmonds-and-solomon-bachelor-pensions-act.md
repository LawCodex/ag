## SCHEDULE

ST. CLAIR SIMMONDS AND SOLOMON BACHELOR
PENSIONS

(22nd July, 1967.)

WHEREAS the officers named in the Schedule to this
Act were employed at Government House as personal
attendants of the Governor of Antigua and the other Presidencies of the Leeward Islands Colony before they were placed
on the pensionable establishment of that Colony:
AND WHEREAS the Government of Antigua has
agreed that it is just and expedient to make provisions for
the payment by the Government of Antigua of a pension
in respect of the period during which they served the Governor of Antigua and the other Presidencies of the Leeward
Islands as aforesaid.
