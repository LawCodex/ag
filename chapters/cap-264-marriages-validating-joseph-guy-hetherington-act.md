### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Marriages (Validating) short title.
Joseph Guy Hetherington Act.
t

### 2.

All marriages solemnized in Antigua and Barbuda
by the Reverend Joseph Guy Hetherington, between the first
day of November and the fifteenth day of December, 1931
(inclusive), are hereby declared to be valid for all intents
and purposes:
Provided that nothing in this Act shall be deemed to
validate any marriage which is invalid for any reason save
that the said Reverend Joseph Guy Hetherington was not
duly appointed a marriage officer at the time that he solemnized such marriage.
