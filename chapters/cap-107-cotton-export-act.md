Penalty.

### 5.

Any person who acts in contravention of section 3
shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on summary
conviction to a fine not exceeding ten thousand dollars or
to imprisonment with or without hard labour for a term not
exceeding six months or to both such fine and imprisonment.
