OBJECTIVES
1.1

To establish through a public company an investment fund or series

and controlled companies and corporations located in the Signatory

fostering import substitution and or increasing hard currency earnings and or increasing production of goods or services which are projected to stimulate economic growth in the Signatory States in which
the investment and or funding is made.
