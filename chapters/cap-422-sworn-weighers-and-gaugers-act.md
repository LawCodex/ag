### 9.

If any person shall fraudulently forge or counterfeit
the brand or mark on any package or cask of any weigher or
gauger licensed under this Act, or shall fraudulently alter
any such mark, every such person shall be liable on conviction before a Magistrate to be imprisoned with or without
hard labour for any period not exceeding six months.
