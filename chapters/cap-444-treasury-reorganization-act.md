Every right, power, privilege, immunity or duty
which immediately before the commencement of this Act
was or was capable of being exercised, enjoyed, or performed by the Treasurer by virtue of any instrument having
the force of law in Antigua and Barbuda, not being an Act
or Ordinance enacted by the Legislature, shall, from and
after the commencement of this Act in relation to Antigua
and Barbuda be exercised, enjoyed and performed by the
Accountant General.
