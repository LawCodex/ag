### 9.

Any rum sold, disposed of or dealt with contrary
to the provisions of this Act shall be forfeited. And any person who sells, buys, disposes of or deals with rum contrary
to the provisions of this Act shall be liable on summary conviction to pay a penalty not exceeding two hundred and fifty
dollars.
