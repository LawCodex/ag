VILLA CHURCH OF CHRIST (INCORPORATION)
(19th August, 1982.)

WHEREAS there have existed congregations of
Churches in the world including the United States of
America called Churches of Christ:

AND WHEREAS missionaries from the United States of
America have commenced a mission in Antigua and
Barbuda to preach the gospel of Christ amongst the people
of the said Country:
AND WHEREAS the said missionaries have established
a church in Villa in the Parish of Saint John called the Villa
Church of Christ:

AND WHEREAS for the purposes of the said Church
and its work it is desirable and expedient that the said Villa
Church of Christ should have the right to acquire, hold and

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Villa Church of Christ
(Incorporation) Act.

The Villa Church
of Christ
constituted a
body corporate.

### 2.

The Villa Church of Christ is hereby constituted a
body corporate under the name and title of the Villa
Church of Christ with perpetual succession and a Common
Seal and with power and authority by such name aforesaid
to sue and be sued, and to acquire and hold property of
whatever kind for any uses and purposes thereof, and to sell,
mortgage, lease, exchange or otherwise dispose of the said
property as they see fit.

Execution and
subscription of
deeds etc.

### 3.

All transfers, conveyances, mortgages, cancellation
of mortgages, leases, contracts, assignments or other deeds
and documents whatsoever required to be executed by the
said Villa Church of Christ shall be deemed sufficiently executed if sealed with its Common Seal and subscribed to by
its Chairman and Secretary or Treasurer for the time being.

Senice of
process.

### 4.

In all legal proceedings against the Villa Church of
Christ service of process shall be deemed good and sufficient if served upon the Chairman, Secretary or Treasurer
for the time being, or in the event of there being no such
persons, if affixed on a conspicuous part of the Church.

Saving.

### 5.

Nothing in this Act shall prejudice or affect the
rights of Her Majesty the Queen, Her Heirs or Successors or
any body politic or corporate or other person or persons
whatever.
