### 5.

(1) The Committee shall, with a view to the proper
performance of its functions in relation to the Order of National
Hero established under this Act, make such investigations as it
considers proper and necessary to determine persons, living or
dead who may be recognised and, upon whom the honour of
National Hero may be conferred.

(2) The Committee shall, subject to subsection (1) make a
report of the results of its investigations to the Prime Minister who
shall then advise the Governor General accordingly.

### 6.

Any person or organization may submit to the Committee
for its consideration, a nomination of a citizen of Antigua and
Barbuda who, in the opinion of such person or organization, has
performed an heroic act or rendered an heroic service to Antigua
and Barbuda or to humanity at large for recognition and conferment of the honour of National Hero.

### 7.

(1) The Governor General may, subject to section 6, by
instrument under the seal of the Order confer the honour of
National Hero upon any citizen of Antigua and Barbuda who has
performed an heroic act or rendered an heroic service to Antigua
and Barbuda or to humanity at large.

(2) The honour of National Hero may be conferred upon a
person posthumously or during his life time.

(3) Where the instrument by which the honour of a National

Attorney General may, by Order, declare the person named in the
instrument as a National Hero
