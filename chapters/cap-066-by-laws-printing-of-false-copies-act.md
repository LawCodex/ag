## CHAPTER 66

## Arrangement of Sections

Section

BY-LAWS (PRINTING O F FALSE COPIES)
(25th February, 1921.)

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the By-Laws (Printing
of False Copies) Act.

### 2.

Any person who-

(a) prints any paper which falsely purports to be
a copy of a By-law, or,
(b) tenders in evidence any such paper falsely purporting to be a copy of a By-law knowing the same to
be false
shall be guilty of felony and on conviction shall be liable
to be imprisoned for any term not exceeding two years with
or without hard labour.
