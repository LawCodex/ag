### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Provincial Elders'
Conference of the Eastern West Indies Province of the Moravian Church (Incorporation) Act.

### 2.

The Provincial Elders' Conference of the Eastern
West Indies Province of the Moravian Church is hereby constituted a body corporate under the name and title of "The
Provincial Elders' Conference of the Eastern West Indies
Province of the Moravian Church" with perpetual succession and a Common Seal and with power and authority by
that name to sue and be sued, to acquire and to hold real
and personal property for all purposes connected with the
administration of the affairs of the Provincial Elders'
Conference of the Eastern West Indies Province of the
Moravian Church in Antigua and Barbuda and with power
to sell, mortgage, alienate, lease, exchange or otherwise
dispose of the said property.

Execution and
subscription of
deeds etc. by
Chairman.

### 3.

All deeds of conveyance or reconveyance, mortgages, charges, leases, transfers, contracts, assignments or
other deeds and documents whatsoever and all powers of
attorney and all notarial instruments required to be executed
by the said Provincial Elders' Conference of the Eastern West
Indies Province of the Moravian Church shall be deemed
sufficiently executed if sealed with its common seal and
subscribed by the Chairman or Vice Chairman of the Provincial Elders' Conference of the Eastern West Indies Province of the Moravian Church for the time being and such
execution shall be valid and effectual at law.

Vesting of
property.

### 4.

All real and personal property in Antigua and
Barbuda now held by or vested in any person on behalf of
the Provincial Elders' Conference of the Eastern West Indies
Province of the Moravian Church in Antigua and Barbuda
are hereby transferred to and vested in the Provincial Elders'
Conference of the Eastern West Indies Province of the Moravian Church and its successors for the same estates and
interests and to the extent to which the same were respectively at the time of the passing of this Act held or vested

### 5.

Nothing herein contained shall affect or be deemed
to affect the rights of Her Majesty, Her heirs and successors
or any body politic or corporate or of any other persons except
such as are mentioned in this Act and those claiming by,
from or under them.
