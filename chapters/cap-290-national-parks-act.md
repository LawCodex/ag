## SECOND SCHEDULE.

An Act to provide for the establishment of National Parks
and a National Parks Authority; to make provision for
the preservation, protection, management and development of the natural physical and ecological resources
and the historical and cultural heritage of Antigua and
Barbuda; and for matters connected with those purposes.
(13th December, 1984.)
