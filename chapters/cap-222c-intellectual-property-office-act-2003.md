ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as
follows 1. This Act may be cited as the Intellectual Property Office
Act, 2003 and comes into force on a day appointed by the Minister by notice published in the Gazette.

### 2.

In this Act,
"intellectual property legislation"includes the Copyright
Act, 2003, the Geographical Indications Act, 2003, the
Industrial Designs Act, 2003, the Integrated Circuits Topography Act, 2003, the Patents Act, 2003, the Trademarks Act, 2003, and any other Act which the Minister
may by Order designate;
"Minister" means the Minister responsible for the administration of Intellectual Property Legislation;
"Registrar" means the Registrar of Intellectual Property
appointed under section 4.

### 3.

(1) There is established an Office to be known as the Intellectual Property Office.
(2) The Registrar is responsible for the administration of the
Intellectual Property Office and shall be assisted by a Deputy
Registrar and such officers as may be appointed.

### 4.

It shall be lawful fqr ,the Public Service Commission to
appoint on such terms and conditions as it may deternine, a
Registrar and a Deputy Registrar of Intellectual Property Office for Antigua and Barbuda and such officers to assist the
Registrar of Intellectual Property as the Public Service Commission may consider necessary.

### 5.

The salaries and allowances to be paid to the Registrar,
the Deputy Registrar and other officers appointed under section 4 shall be paid out of moneys voted by Parliament.

Duties of the Registrar of Intellectual Property.

### 6.

(1) The Registrar shall (a)

perform all functions relating to (i)

the grant of patents and utility model certificates;

(ii)

the registration of industrial designs, marks,
collective marks and geographical indications;

(b)

superintend and perform such other duties and exercise such other powers as are conferred by the
intellectual property legislation or the regulations
made thereunder; and

(c)

be responsible for the carrying out of studies,
programmes or exchanges of items or services regarding domestic and international intellectual property issues and regarding the use of patent documents as a source of information.

(2) Decisions of the Registrar shall be signed by the Registrar or in the absence of the Registrar, the Deputy Registrar or
an officer designated by the Registrar.

(1) The Registrar may, subject to any provision in any
intellectual property legislation or regulations made under such
legislation, correct any error of translation or transcription, clerical error or mistake in any application or document filed with
