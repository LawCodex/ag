### 2.

It shall not be lawful for any person to introduce
into the Island of Barbuda any mongoose; and any person prohibited.
who introduces or attempts to introduce or is concerned in
introducing into the said Island of Barbuda any mongoose,
or who knowingly harbours or keeps any mongoose in the
said Island, shall be liable on conviction before a Magistrate
to be imprisoned for a period not exceeding six months, or
to pay a penalty not exceeding five thousand dollars.

### 4.

If shall be lawful for the Secretary of the Barbuda
Council or for any Justice of the Peace, or police officer,
or any person acting in aid or under the direction of the

during the day-time any place or premises, and to search
for, seize and destroy any mongoose found therein.
