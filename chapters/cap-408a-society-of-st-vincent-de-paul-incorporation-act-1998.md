AN ACT to provide for the incorporationof the Society of St.

[ 8th October, 1998 ]

WHEREAS the Society of St. Vincent de Paul has been serving
Antigua and Barbuda since 1975 and in 1989 appointed its first
Antiguan and Barbudan Board Trustees to further develop the
mission and progress of the Society; and
WHEREAS the Society acquired lands situate in Golden Grove
and Michael's Village on which it administers charitable services
and humanitarian assistance to the sick, aged and economically
disadvantaged persons and currently the Society operates the Good
Shepherd Home for Children and the St. Vincent de Paul Elderly
Day Care Centre; and
WHEREAS it is desirable and expedient that the Society of St.
Vincent de Paul shall have the right to acquire, hold, deal with and
dispose of movable and immovable property in Antigua and
Barbuda for purposes of the work of the Society.
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as
follows:
