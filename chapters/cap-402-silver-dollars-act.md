# Silver Dollars Act

(CAP. 402

## CHAPTER 402

T H E SILVER DOLLARS ACT

## Arrangement of Sections

Section
1\. Short title.
2\. Silver dollars demonetized.

SILVER DOLLARS

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Silver Dollars Act. Short title.

Silver dollars

### 2.

In all payments to be made in Antigua and Barbuda demonetized.
tender of payments in the Spanish, Mexican or Columbian
silver coins called "dollars" shall cease to be deemed and
taken to be a legal tender.
