AN ACT to provide for the payment of Pension Benefit to James
Drew (et al).
[ 25th February, 1999 ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as
follows1. This Act may be cited as the James Drew (et al) ( Pension
Benefit) Act, 1999.

### 2.

(1)

Notwithstanding any law to the contrary, authority is
hereby given for the payment out of the Consolidated
Fund the sumof Seven thousand, two hundredEastern
Caribbean dollars ($7,200) per annum as pension to
the persons named in the Schedule.

(2)

The pension payable under subsection (1) shall be
paid monthly in arrears in equal installments.

### 3.

(1)

The pension payable under this Act shall commence
on the 1st day of January, 1999 until the death of the
person named in the Schedule.

(2)

Where a person named in the Schedule dies, his
widow shall be paid a pension at the annual rate of two
thirds of the sum paid to the person so named.

### 4.

The pension payable under this Act shall not(a)

be assignable or transferableexcept for the purpose of
satisfying a debt due to the Government or an Order
of a Court towards the maintenance of a minor child
of a person named in the Schedule, or

(b)

be liable to be attached, sequestered or levied upon for
or in respect of any debt or claim whatever except a
debt due to the Government or any sum recoverable
pursuant to such order of any court as mentioned in
sub-paragraph (a).
