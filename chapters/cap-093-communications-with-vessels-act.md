### 2.

Any person who-

(a) not being a licensed pilot and acting in that
capacity, shall approach nearer to any vessel arriving
in Antigua and Barbuda than a distance of approximately fifty yards, or shall board or leave such vessel; or

(b) being the master or commander of any vessel
arriving in Antigua and Barbuda shall allow any person to board or leave such vessel,
before the vessel shall have been boarded by the harbour
master or visiting officer and before leave shall have been
granted by the harbour master or visiting officer for persons
to board or leave the vessel, shall be guilty of an offence
