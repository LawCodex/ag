## SCHEDULE

The money borrowed under the authority of this Act shall when approved by
Cabinet from time to time, be applied to development projects in Antigua and
Barbuda, including but not limited to feasibility studies, project evaluation and
preparation, construction of projects, and any other matter or matters incidental
thereto.
