ST. JOHN'S CHURCHYARD
(20th July, 1880.)

Whereas the ancient churchyard surrounding the Preamble.
Cathedral of St. John's is no longer used as a burial ground
and whereas it is expedient that the public should have free
access to the same and that the churchyard and the walls
surrounding it should be kept in good order and repair and
whereas the Church Commissioners have consented to surrender the churchyard to the public on the condition that
the churchyard and the walls surrounding the same should
be put in good order and repair and so maintained at the
public expense.
