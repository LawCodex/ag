### 4.

(1) It shall be lawful for the Commissioners to
employ a competent Surveyor or Surveyors to make plans of
such streets and sidewalks as aforesaid, and to take measurements of the same, and to do such other work in that behalf
as the Commissioners may require; and the Commissioners
or any of them, and such Surveyor or Surveyors as aforesaid,
in and for the carrying out of the work mentioned in section
3 may enter upon any house, building, yard or premises
abutting on the said streets and sidewalks at all reasonable
hours, and therein to take such measurements and do all
such things as may be necessary for the effectual carrying
through of such work as aforesaid.
(2) Any person who shall without reasonable excuse
obstruct, hinder or refuse entrance to the said
Commissioners and Surveyors or any of them shall be guilty
of an offence under this Act, and shall be liable upon conviction before a Magistrate to pay a sum not exceeding two
hundred and fifty dollars, and in default of such payment to
be dealt with by such Magistrate under the Magistrate's Code
of Procedure Act.

### 5.

The proceedings of the Commissioners shall be as

follows(1) They shall cause to be made a plan of each street
drawn to scale, showing the sidewalks thereof and such parts
of the premises of all adjoining owners as are abutting upon
such streets and sidewalks, and shall colour such plan in
three colours, showing a different colour for such streets,
sidewalks and premises as aforesaid respectively, and they
shall also upon such plan mark with a strong red line the
boundary between such premises as aforesaid, and such
streets or sidewalks as aforesaid which they shall determine it
would be expedient to take as the proper boundary between
such premises as aforesaid and such streets and sidewalks as
aforesaid.

(2) Where it shall appear upon such plans that the
premises of such adjoining owners as aforesaid are extending
beyond such red boundary line as aforesaid, such part so
extending as aforesaid shall be clearly shown as and being an
encroachment.
(3) The Commissioners shall draw up a report to be
submitted to the Cabinet, together with such plan as aforesaid, and shall therein make their recommendations as to
whether all or any (if any), and which of the encroachments
shown on the plan should in their opinion be permitted to
remain undisturbed.
(4) Thereupon it shall be lawful for the Cabinet to
cause a copy of such plans as aforesaid to be posted on some
convenient place in the Court House at St. John's, and to
cause a copy of such recommendations as aforesaid to be
delivered to the Central Board of Health and to be published in the Gazette.

(5) Any person objecting to the report shall within one
month from the date of the publication thereof in the Gazette
forward his objections and the grounds thereof in writing to
the Clerk to Parliament.

(6) It shall be lawful for the Cabinet on the expiration
of the month in subsection ( 5 ) mentioned to lay the whole
of the proceedings before Parliament, who shall report what
lines ought in the public interests to be finally taken as the
boundary lines between the premises of adjoining owners as
aforesaid and such streets and sidewalks as aforesaid. The
Cabinet may thereupon cause such action to be taken as may
be necessary:
Provided that nothing herein shall be construed to
affect any right of the Crown or any person at once to proceed to enforce or protect any right in due course of law.

(7) A second set of plans shall be prepared showing the
boundary lines recommended to be taken as the proper
boundary lines as in the last subsection mentioned, and such
plans shall show clearly in three colours the roads, sidewalks
and adjoining premises as recommended, and the plans
aforesaid or certified copies thereof shall be lodged with and

### 10.

Where the line of the street has been finally
determined under the provisions of this Act, any person
who fails to remove or builds or commences to build, or
repairs or commences to repair, any house, wall, gallery,
fence, staircase or other structure, or fails to remove or lays,
or commences to lay, any stone or pavement from, upon,

beyond, or projecting in any way across the line of the street
so determined as aforesaid shall on complaint made by the
Commissioner of Police be liable on summary conviction to
pay a fine not exceeding one thousand dollars, and shall also
be liable to pay a further penalty not exceeding one hundred
dollars for every day during which the matter or thing
complained of is suffered by him to continue unaltered.
