### 2.

The General Secretary of Foreign Missions for the
time being of the General Board of the Pilgrim Holiness
Church shall be and is hereby constituted a body politic and
corporate under the name and title of "The Pilgrim Holiness
Church" with perpetual succession and a Common Seal and
with power and authority by that name to sue and be sued
to acquire and hold real and personal property in trust for
all purposes connected with the administration of the affairs
of the Pilgrim Holiness Church in Antigua and Barbuda and
with power to sell, mortgage, alienate or lease the said
property:
Provided that all lands acquired, held and dealt with
by the said The Pilgrim Holiness Church shall be acquired,
held and dealt with in accordance with the provisions of the
said Non-Citizens Land Holding Regulation Act and if not
so acquired held and dealt with shall be forfeited to
Her Majesty as in the said Non-citizens Land Holding
Regulation Act.
