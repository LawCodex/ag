### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Turtle Act.

Short title.

### 2.

In this Act-

Interpretation.

"turtle" shall be deemed not to include the Loggerhead
Turtle ( Thalassochelys caretta);
"tortoise" shall include land turtle.

### 3.

( a ) catches or takes, or attempts to catch or take,
