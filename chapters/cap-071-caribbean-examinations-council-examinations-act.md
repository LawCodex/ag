### 8.

No person shall forge or alter, or offer, utter,
dispose of or put. off, knowing the same to be forged or
altered, any certificate, purporting to show that a person
has passed any examination held by the Council.

### 9.

A prosecution for an offence under section 6 may
not be instituted except with the consent of the Director of
Public Prosecutions.

Offences and
Penalties.

### 10.

Any person who contravenes or fails to comply
with any of the provisions of this Act is guilty of an offence
and is liable on summary conviction to a fine of ten thousand dollars and to imprisonment for a term of twelve
months.
