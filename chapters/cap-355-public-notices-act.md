### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Public Notices Act. Short title.

### 2.

In this Act-

Interpretation.

"document" includes any application, appointment, bylaw, jury list, minute of proceedings, notification,
notice, order, proceedings, request, return, or rule.
"local newspaper" means any newspaper published in
Antigua and Barbuda.

### 4.

Every such order of the Cabinet may be in either
special or general terms and shall be published in the Gazette;
and every publication and advertisement made in pursuance
of such order shall have the same force and effect as if the
same had been published or advertised in a local newspaper
as originally directed by the Act, by-law, regulation, or
instrument requiring the same to be published or advertised.
