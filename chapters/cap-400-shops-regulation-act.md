### 4.

No shop assistant shall be employed in or about
any shop except during the time fixed by any Shop Hours
Order for the time being in force under this Act.

### 5.

The number of hours during which any shop
assistant may be employed in any shop shall not exceed in
the aggregate in any week forty-five hours exclusive of the
time allowed for meals except as is hereinafter provided:
Provided that a shop assistant may be employed for
stock-taking for a period not exceeding five hours in any
one week in a year which period of five hours shall be in
addition to the time during which he may otherwise be lawfully employed.
