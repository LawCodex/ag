to the credit of a fund to be called the Village Fund.
(2) The moneys paid into the Treasury by any village

or group of adjacent villages as the case may be.

( a ) the moneys derived from any licences, rates,
taxes, fees, dues or charges imposed and recovered
under any By-Law made by the village council;

### 11.

An officer or member of a village council shall
not be personally liable for any loss or damage caused by
any act or omission in the administration of the affairs of
the village council unless such loss or damage shall be occasioned by an intentionally wrongful act on his part:
Provided that nothing in this section shall be construed
as relieving a village council from any liability in respect of
acts or omissions of their officers or members.
