### 4.

Nothing in section 6 (a) or 8 of the Pensions Act
(as enacted by this Act) applies to an officer in the public
service who retired from the public service before the first
day of September, 1984, notwithstanding that such an officer
may have been re-employed in the public service after his
retirement.
