# Caribbean Free Trade Association (Ratification of Agreements) Act

Caribbean Free Trade Association
(Ratification of Agreements)

(CAP. 73

## CHAPTER 73*

T H E CARIBBEAN FREE TRADE ASSOCIATION
(RATIFICATION O F AGREEMENTS) ACT

CARIBBEAN FREE T R A D E ASSOCIATION
(RATIFICATON O F AGREEMENTS)
(30th April, 1968.)

411968.

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Caribbean Free Trade Short title.
Association (Ratification of Agreements) Act.

### 2.

The Agreement signed on the 15th December, Ratification.
1965, at Dickenson Bay, Antigua and Barbuda for the
Establishment of a Caribbean Free Trade Association, and
the Supplementary Agreement amending the same and
rescinding a previous Supplementary Agreement thereto
signed on behalf of the Governments of Antigua and
Barbuda, Barbados and Guyana, are hereby ratified.

\*This Act was repealed by necessary implication upon the coming into
operation of the Caribbean Free Trade Association Ordinance, (Repeal) Act,
1968-No. 11 of 1968.
