ANTIGUA AND BARBUDA
No. 15 of 2004

AN ACT to repeal the Asian Village Resort (Incentives) Act,
1997.
[Published in the Official Gazette
Vol. XXIV No. 83 dated 1lth November, 2004. ]
ENACTED by the Parliament of Antigua and Barbuda as
follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Asian Village Resort
(Incentives) (Repeal) Act, 2004.

### 2.

The Asian Village Resort (Incentives)Act, 1997 is repealed.
Passed by the House of Representatives
this 23rd day of September, 2004.

Shorttitle.

Repeal of Act
No. 14 of 1997.

Passed by the Senate this 7th day
of October, 2004.
