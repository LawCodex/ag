(1) The Board shall consist of a Chairman, a Deputy

forthwith cause it to be forwarded to the Minister.

(5) The Chairman may resign his office by writing addressed
to the Minister.
(6) A resignation by the Chairman or a member of the Board
shall be effective from the date the Minister communicates the

(1)The Board shall meet at least once every month and shall

(2)The Chairman or in his absence the Deputy Chairman shall
preside at every meeting of the Board and in the absence of both
the Chairman and Deputy Chairman the members present shall
appoint a member to preside at the meeting.

(3) The quorum at each meeting of the Board is five and if a
quorum is present, the Board is not disqualifiedfor the transaction
of business by reason of a vacancy among its members.
(4) Decisions of the Board shall be by a majority of votes and
where the votes are equal the chairman shall have acasting vote.

(5) The secretary shall keep a minute book in which the
proceedings at meetings and transactions of the Board shall be
recorded.
