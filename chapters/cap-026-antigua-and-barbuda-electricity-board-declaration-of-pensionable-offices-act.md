### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Antigua and Barbuda
Electricity Board (Declaration of Pensionable Offices) Act.

### 2.

In this Act the expression "the Antigua and Interpretation.
Barbuda Electricity Board" means the Board established 1'1948.
under section 3 of the Electricity Ice and Cold Storage
Ordinance, 1948, since repealed.

CAP. 26) Antigua and Barbuda Electricity Board
(Declaration of Pensionable Offices)

Service under the
Antigua and
Barbuda
Electricity Board
prior to 1st
January, 1957,
deemed to be
service in a
pensionable
office.

### 3.

For the purpose of computing the pensions and
gratuities under the Pensions Act, or any law amending,
repealing, or substituted for the same, the persons mentioned in the Schedule (and of such other former employees of
the Antigua and Barbuda Electricity Board as the GovernorGeneral, in his discretion, may from time to time direct),
the service of each of those persons prior to the 1st day of
January, 1957, under the Antigua and Barbuda Electricity
Board shall be deemed to have been service in a pensionable
office under the Government.
