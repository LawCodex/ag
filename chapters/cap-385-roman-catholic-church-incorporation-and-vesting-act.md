### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Roman Catholic
Church Incorporation and Vesting Act.

### 2.

The Right Reverend Joseph Oliver Bowers S.V.D.,
D.D. The Roman Catholic Bishop of St. John's and his
successors in office shall be and is hereby constituted a
corporation under the style and title of the "Bishop of
St. John's" with perpetual succession and a common seal
and authority by that corporate name to sue and be sued
to acquire and hold real and personal property in trust for
all purposes connected with the affairs of the Roman Catholic
Church in Antigua and Barbuda and with power to sell,
mortgage, alienate or lease the said property.

Vesting of
Property.

### 3.

All real and personal property in Antigua and
Barbuda now held or vested in the Bishop of Roseau or held
by or vested in any person or persons on behalf of the Roman
Catholic Church Parishioners in Antigua and Barbuda are
hereby transferred to and vested in the Bishop of St. John's
and his successors and assigns for the same estates and
interests and to the extent to which the same were respectively
at the time of the passing of this Act held or vested as aforesaid
subject to the rights, reservations and charges (if any) now
affecting the same and the trusts upon which the same are
now held.

said corporation.
