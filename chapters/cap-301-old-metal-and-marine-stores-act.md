### 6.

(1) No person shall purchase or receive any old
metal or marine stores except between the hours of seven dealings.
o'clock in the morning and six o'clock in the evening.
(2) No person shall purchase or receive any old metal
or marine stores from any person under the age of sixteen
years.
(3) No person shall employ any servant or apprentice
or other person under the age of sixteen years to purchase
or receive any old metal or marine stores.

(4) No old metal or marine stores shall be purchased
or received at any one time in any quantity less than twentyfive pounds.

(5) Any person contravening any of the provisions of
this section shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable
on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding five hundred dollars or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding
six months.
