(4) Where on proceedings under this section the jury
find that the woman in question is not pregnant, the woman
may appeal to the Court of Appeal, and that Court, if satisfied
that for any reason the finding should be set aside, shall quash
the sentence passed on her and instead thereof pass on her
a sentence of imprisonment for life.
